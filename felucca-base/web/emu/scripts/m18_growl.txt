# Demo 8 (M18 AMB GROWL, the deep growl drone): its MULTIS page and 6 s of audio with no key held, then with G3 held.
boot
master 800
button SAVE
enc K1 -20
enc K1 17
shot m18_page
button OCTUP
wait 600
rec m18_nokey
wait 6000
rec off
keys G3
wait 500
rec m18_g3
wait 6000
rec off
keys off
button HOME
shot m18_home

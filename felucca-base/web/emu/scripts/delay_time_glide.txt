# Demo 7 (DLYGLIDE): a delay TIME change glides in the modulated delay. M02 DUB CHORD (DUB knob set: K4 is TIME), chord held, TIME stepped while 4 s are recorded: soundlab measure DIR/glide_up, and compare with a render of soundlab --fx-time-step.
boot
master 800
button SAVE
enc K1 -20
enc K1 1
button OCTUP
wait 600
button HOME
keys C4 Eb4 G4
wait 800
shot time_before
rec glide_up
wait 1500
enc K4 4
wait 2500
rec off
shot time_after
rec glide_down
wait 1500
enc K4 -4
wait 2500
rec off
keys off

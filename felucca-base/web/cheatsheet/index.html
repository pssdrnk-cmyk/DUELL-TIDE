Part marker

Getting around

Turn SELECT
Switches between the pages of a module; in lists and the menu it moves the row.
Tap FX / SEL / ENV / LFO / EDIT / GLO / ARP / SEQ
Switches between the modules.
Tap HOME
MORPH page. Tap again for TIDE SETTINGS and AUTO TIDE (the TIDE sections below).
Hold HOME
Opens the menu. HOME closes it.
Press ALGORITHM
Selects the part you edit. On the MORPH page and on PERFORM: PART 1, the middle (both parts, in the PLAY MODE row's mode), PART 2.
Hold EDIT / LFO / ENV / ARP / GLO / SEQ / REC
Quick page: the four knobs become performance controls. Let go to go back.
Hold FX
PERFORM page. Tap FX to leave.
Hold OCT- + turn a knob
Resets that knob's card to its default. In a dialog or menu OCT- means back / no, OCT+ do it / yes.

Playing

Tap OCT- / OCT+
Octave down / up of the keys. Both together reset the octave to 0.
Tap REC
Not armed: HOLD for the part(s) the keys play (on HOME too; to record from HOME, hold SEQ and tap REC). Once a part is armed, a REC tap disarms it on every page, HOME included, and leaves HOLD alone.
Tap PLAY/STOP
Stopped: starts when you let go. Running: stops at once.
Hold PLAY/STOP, or press it twice
STOP ALL: silences everything, delay, chorus and reverb tails too.
Hold GLO + OCT- / OCT+
Mutes / unmutes part 1 / part 2.
Mod wheel (CC1)
Moves the MORPH fader.

Sound

MORPH page
Knob 1 glides from End A to End B. Hold OCT+ and click knob 1 to roll a new End B; a roll replaces that end (no UNDO). Knobs 2 to 4: FILTER, RESONANCE, ECHO for a single sound; on a multi the set of its genre (the KNOBS row below). End B is saved exactly as it sounds, with the patch.

Sequencer / patterns

Turn a knob with REC armed
Records a parameter lock. Tap SEQ, turn SELECT to PARAMETER LOCKS to change or delete them.
Hold SEQ
QUICK SEQ tools on the white keys from F3.

Saving & loading

Tap SAVE
Opens the page that fits: PATTERNS on a sequencer page, otherwise MULTIS in LAYER and PATCHES in DUEL and STEREO. SELECT moves between PATCHES, PATTERNS, MULTIS, TOOLS.
Hold SAVE
Overwrites the slot your sound came from; OCT+ yes, OCT- no. On the MORPH page with TARGET BOTH, each part goes over its own slot.
MIDI program change
LAYER: program 0 to 25 on part 1's channel loads multis M01 to M26 (bank 0 only; 0 to 15 and M01 to M16 on plain DUELL). DUEL and STEREO: bank 0, program n loads entry n of the PATCHES list; bank 1, program n loads patch slot n.
Backup (installer page or editor Backup page)
Saves patches, multis, patterns, user wavetables and settings. A flash keeps your saved data; back up before every flash. Room for 128 patches, 16 multis (M01 to M16), 32 patterns (P01 to P32).

Good to know

  • The PATCH LEVEL is saved with the patch; the PART LEVEL on the mixer is yours.
  • KEY VELOCITY and AFTERTOUCH are on by default; switch them off in MENU > CONTROL.
  • Teach a CC with MIDI LEARN on the unit. CC1 mod wheel, CC11 expression, CC64 sustain pedal are inputs.

TIDE: the round on HOME

Tap HOME
MORPH, then TIDE SETTINGS, then AUTO TIDE, one tap each. ALGORITHM picks PART 1, the middle or PART 2 on all of them: the middle is both parts, playing the PLAY MODE row's LAYER, STEREO or DUEL (the header names it).
Pills beside the capsules
Dim pill by part 1: busiest audio block of the last 4 s, in % of its time. Red badge by part 2 (only after a miss): audio blocks missed recently; it goes away after a minute with no new miss.
MORPH page
The hands-on page: K1 the fader A to B (two steps a click), K2 FILTER, K3 RESONANCE, K4 ECHO (a multi with a genre tag in its name, such as DUB or ACID, gets that genre's macros instead: the KNOBS row). With both parts selected every knob moves each part by the same amount. Hold OCT+ and click K1: a new End B (no UNDO). OCT- + a knob: K1 back to the original sound, K2 to K4 back to the loaded sound's value.

TIDE: AUTO TIDE

K1 GLIDE
Twist right to go to B, left to go to A; it starts on the next bar. Twist two detents while it runs and it stops where it is.
K2 MODE (AUTO TIDE mode)
What happens at each arrival: EVOLVE or TRAVEL.
K3 TIME
Bars for one trip: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64.
K4 WAIT
Bars to hold at each arrival: OFF, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16.
Hold OCT- + K1 / K2 / K3 / K4
K1: back HOME to your original sound and pattern. K2: drops the ARC at the next 4-bar phrase (a second turn cancels it). K3 and K4: TIME and WAIT go back to the STYLE's values (DUB: 16 bars and 2 bars).
  • TRAVEL travels through factory sounds of the part's own engine and stays in the starting sound's role (pads and keys, bass, lead and pluck, hits, FX); it never changes engine.
  • EVOLVE walks every dial on from your original, one step per trip, the step growing with the phrase length (2 to 8 sixteenths of AMOUNT); a dial turns round at the edge of AMOUNT, and some dials wait for longer phrases.

TIDE: TIDE SETTINGS

K1 picks the row
OCT+ the next value, OCT- the one before. Saved after about two seconds, while stopped.
STYLE
DUB (the default), DEEP, MINIMAL, AMBIENT, PEAK, DRONE, CUSTOM. One pick sets MODE, TIME, WAIT and ARC, and how the pattern varies, takes turns, drifts against the beat and breathes; changing MODE, TIME, WAIT or ARC by hand makes it CUSTOM.
MODE
The AUTO TIDE mode, as K2 there: EVOLVE or TRAVEL.
TARGET
BOTH, SOUND or SEQ: what the glide moves.
ARC
OFF, RISE or DROP: the long shape of rise, peak and breakdown. Acts in automatic EVOLVE and TRAVEL.
KNOBS
Which set K2, K3 and K4 play on HOME page 1 (K1 is always MORPH): AUTO (reads the first word of the multi's name), STD, DUB, HSE, TECH, ACID, ELEC, AMB. Every new End B is rolled MUSIC.
AMOUNT
0 to 100 %: how far a roll may go (the MORPH page's knob 4 until 1.0.0-b7). Not part of a STYLE.
ANSWER / ANSWER ON
When a recording pass closes, the other part is given a bass line in the key (BASS LINE, the default) or your notes a third above (DOUBLE), or a REPLY that ends on the tonic, or an ECHO of your last note (the last two double the loop: a call of 2 bars at most); OFF keeps nothing. ANSWER ON: OTHER (the other part) or OFF. OCT- held + a PRESETS turn goes back along the last 2 takes (left) or forward (right); ROLL on the part that holds the answer writes it again.
REC
ADD (the default) or REPLACE: when you record live into a step that holds notes, a new note is added to them, or the first note of the pass takes their place (a chord played together is kept). On an ARP SEQ part a key sets the step's note (one note a step). Not part of a STYLE.
PLAY MODE / XFADE / SPLIT
The last three rows: LAYER, STEREO or DUEL; the velocity crossfade; the key split (OFF, MIDDLE, then a note). PLAY MODE is what ALGORITHM's middle plays (changing it moves ALGORITHM there). XFADE acts in the middle in LAYER; SPLIT in the middle in any mode. Knobs 2 to 4 move them by the clicks you turn; OCT+ / OCT- step XFADE 10 % and SPLIT an octave.

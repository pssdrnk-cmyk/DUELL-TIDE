TIDE

TIDE is a morph that plays and evolves by itself.

The MORPH glides a sound between two versions of itself, End A and End B. TIDE moves that fader for you, in time with the music, and each trip comes out a little different. Set a tide going, then play your drum machine, bass or sampler over it.

Use at your own risk. TIDE has run on one FM-1, the fork owner's. It has not been tested widely. The FM-1 has a single copy of its firmware and no known rollback. A failed write can leave it dark, and then only a hardware recovery helps (see the recovery notes). Back up first. No warranty.

What TIDE does

The glide runs from A to B and back, never quite the same way twice. It is made for playing the FM-1 as a background: nothing has to be managed live, and a hand on the knob can take over at any time. The pattern can glide with the sound.

Screenshots from the web emulator.

Five modes

  • ONCE: one trip, then it stops.
  • LOOP: back and forth between A and B, the same two ends every time.
  • DRIFT: new twists on your sound, never far from the original.
  • WNDR: wanders away from your sound, one step per trip, the steps growing with the phrase.
  • JRNY: travels through the factory sounds, on the part's own engine.

Play a tide in four steps

  1. Press HOME until AUTO TIDE shows.
  2. Pick a MODE with knob 2, a trip length (TIME, in bars) with knob 3 and a rest at each end (WAIT) with knob 4. Turn ALGORITHM to BOTH to glide both parts at once.
  3. Twist knob 1 right (to B) or left (to A) and press PLAY. While the music plays, the glide starts on the next bar.
  4. Play over it. STOP pauses the glide and PLAY goes on from there. Twist knob 1 two detents while it glides and it stops where it is.

On the very first start TIDE comes up ready: multi M02 DUB CHORD, AUTO TIDE on JRNY, both parts, 16 bars, WAIT 2. Just press PLAY.

TIDE lets the FM-1 play itself. A sound glides from its End A to an End B and back, never quite the same way twice, and in JRNY it travels on through other sounds while the pattern changes with it. Set it going and play over it. Everything else is DUELL 1.1 by OSCILL/R.

Open the web emulator

No FM-1 needed. Press HOME until AUTO TIDE, then twist KNOB 1.

# Allow-list review, 12 November 2026 (b10 round 5, K6 follow-up)

The 135 entries of `felucca-base/tools/ram_allow.py` that said "mix or effect stage ... not yet reviewed" (and the 19 FM6 / morph / twocore helpers added later) are reviewed. No firmware change; only the reasons changed.

- **The 116 that [REPORT.md](REPORT.md) section 3 classified** (b3, 8 November): each reason now carries its class (per block, per sample when on, event), when it is on, its size and the report's verdict. M1, M2 and L are **RAM candidates** (44 with the six below); F and N **stay in the flash** with the reason (per sample but only with a master / DUEL / feature setting on, a mode no factory multi uses, a transient, an event or UI).
- **The 19 the report does not cover** (FM6 envelope and live-level helpers, morph crossing curves, twocore, the voice-start queue) were read in the source on 12 November; their reasons say what they do and when.
- Nothing was measured on the unit: the cost of a flash cache-line fill is still the one unknown of the report (section 1). The candidates are ranked by size below; the report's section 4 gives the order by expected gain per byte (executed bytes that are on in most scenes). Room today: hot block 244 B free, range Z about 8.7 KB above the stack guard (568 B above the 8,192 B pool-tail rule).

# Known issues: DUELL with TIDE

What is known to be broken or untested. [KNOWN_LIMITS.md](KNOWN_LIMITS.md) lists the design limits; this page lists the bugs and the untested ground. Report anything else on this fork's [issues](https://github.com/pssdrnk-cmyk/DUELL-TIDE/issues/new/choose). The sections say where each note comes from: the TIDE fork, or the DUELL 1.0 notes of OSCILL/R that still hold.

## TIDE (this fork)

- **In the beta `beta-2026-11-04`, fixed in the next build:** the SAVE > MULTIS page layout, the TIDE page's step bars not always matching what plays, and a latched arp playing on after STOP (use STOP ALL there: a double press or a 0.6 s hold). [The README](../README.md#builds-stable-and-beta) says which build is which.
- **Known and being fixed, not in any build yet:** FM6 pads and keys can click and dip on re-strike, arp and held chords; the arp can drift off the grid over a long set and swing can swing the wrong way; delay TIME and TEMPO moves can click and the tempo is less steady under an external clock; some sounds sit in the wrong JRNY role family or have level data that is off; some TIDE pages and rows do not show the true state.
- **ARC starts again at RISE after every STOP and PLAY**, and a multi or pattern loaded after a WNDR or JRNY session keeps the generated variation until you press HOME. Both are known; changes are planned.
- **TIDE has run on one FM-1, the fork owner's.** Other units, cables and USB hubs are untested. Use a data cable straight into the computer, no hub.
- **TIDE's settings are not in the backup yet.** The TIDE SETTINGS rows are kept on the unit between power-ups, but the device backup (installer page, editor Backup page) does not hold them. Note them down before ERASE DATA or a reinstall with the erase box ticked.
- **Tested in software and by ear on one unit.** Host tests, the emulator and the editor's mock unit show that the code does what it says; how a tide sounds with a real MIDI clock has been judged on one unit only.

## Inherited from DUELL 1.0

- **Units tested.** DUELL has run on two FM-1 units, the author's, and more than 100 people have installed it through the Firmware Hub.
- **The way back to the official firmware** (V15) has worked on the author's unit about three times: the installer's return button wrote V15 and the unit booted normally. Other units are untested. Single-bank layout, no known rollback beyond that. See [RECOVERY.md](RECOVERY.md).
- **The editor and restore are not tested with many units.** The web editor (patch library, backup and restore, piano roll, wavetable files) and the installer's backup and restore have been used on the author's unit; other units, other computers and other USB-MIDI setups are untested, and the piano roll has not been used on a real unit yet. The backup covers patches, multis, patterns, the user wavetables, settings and the current music; restoring a backup on another unit has not been tried.
- **The 128-patch export and import are untested on hardware.** The editor moves a whole patch with PATCH_RAW (in pieces over USB-MIDI). The USB-MIDI timing of a 128-patch export or import, the flash timing of 128 writes and playing while importing have been tested on the computer only (simulated flash and unit), not measured on a real unit. Do not import a whole bank while you play.
- **Very rare missed audio blocks.** When you switch to a very heavy multi, a block of audio can be late once in a while; you may hear a short click. Normal loads of patches and multis do not do this.
- **The CPU figures in the documentation are a host model; the unit can read higher on some scenes.** The percentages in TESTING.md, the bench and the changelog are instruction counts on a computer. A count of the device code (not a measurement) says the part EQ costs about three times its model figure on the unit, the SHIM pitch shifter and the RANDOM delay about 1.5 times, and the flash cache adds an unknown part. M16 STRESS TEST of 0.2.23 overloaded at one key on the author's unit where the model said 78 %; 0.2.24 recalibrated it (TESTING.md "CPU on the unit"). Read the CPU bar on your unit, not the model.
- **A slot shows the factory sound of the installed firmware.** The 128 patch slots hold the factory sounds until you save over one (RESTORE brings one back; with TIDE the last four, U1 to U4, are empty USER slots, and the factory sounds #125 to #160 are read-only places after the slots). The factory sound a slot falls back to is the one at that place in the list of the firmware you run: when a new version adds or sorts in sounds, a slot you never saved can show another sound than before, while a sound you saved stays in its slot number.
- **With TIDE the patch store holds 126 patches, not 128.** The patch list has 128 slots, but with TIDE the store has 21 chunks of at most 6 records, so it holds at most 126 saved patches, whatever their size. Overwriting a patch already saved always works. Saving into a 127th empty slot is refused: the unit shows STORE FULL, nothing is written and nothing already saved is touched, and the editor's store command reports an error. ERASE or RESTORE a saved slot to make room. Without TIDE the store has 22 chunks and holds all 128. K1 on PATCHES steps one slot a turn (faster with KNOB ACCEL ON), so use TYPE (knob 2: FAV, a category or an engine) to shorten the list. The editor's patch page lists the 128 slots with a search.
- **The second core is new.** Part 2 plays on the chip's second core from every power-on, with an automatic fallback to one core (see [RECOVERY.md](RECOVERY.md)). The two-core firmware has run on the author's unit and the unit fault tests were done; there is no menu switch or on-screen notice for it.
- **MIDI is untested with many devices.** It follows the spec and the host tests; real controllers, sequencers and DAWs are still to be tested.
- **WAVE user tables are new and tested on the computer only.** The three slots (USR1 to USR3) are checked by host tests (flash places, CRC, power cuts, refusal while playing, backup round trip) and the editor's WAVETABLES tab against a simulated unit; the writes have not been tried on other units than the author's, and the editor tab has not been used to write a table on a real unit in a long session. A user table is reduced to 19 frames x 64 harmonics with no phases; a file with a very sharp or noisy sound loses its top and its character. An empty slot plays a sine. ERASE DATA erases the tables.
- **User sample slots:** gone since 0.2.9, with the sample engines. Slot 3's flash now holds the patch store; the WAVE user tables (0.2.14) sit in 24 KiB at the start of the old slot 1 range (0xA0000 to 0xA5FFF); the rest of the old sample flash (0xA6000 to 0xB9FFF) now holds the patch store's chunks 6 to 15 (80 KiB); 0xBA000 to 0xC7FFF (56 KiB) is free.
- **GLIDE and swing do not follow their displays everywhere.** The top third of the GLIDE RATE knob does nothing and TIME is not the same for every interval (glide speed has a limit of about 86 semitones a second). The swing % on screen is a little higher than where the offbeat lands (75 % plays about 70 %). Pressing DUELL's own PLAY while an external clock runs starts at once and loses the master's bar.
- **Version text:** the device shows "DUELL 1.1" (splash, ABOUT). The release has no TEST screen; M16 is a normal musical multi (CELLAR TAPE); the former STRESS TEST is a test scene only.
- **What the web editor cannot set:** the output MODE (DUEL, STEREO, LAYER), BALANCE, CROSSFADE, SPLIT, the MORPH fader and its ROLL / UNDO, SCALE, ROOT and OFF-SCALE, and the parts' MIDI channels. Set them on the unit.

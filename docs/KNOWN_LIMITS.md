# Known limits: DUELL with TIDE

**DUELL** with TIDE: pssdrnk's fork of DUELL 1.1, MultiMorphing Bitimbral Synthesizer by OSCILL/R

The fork's own limits are listed first, then the inherited DUELL 1.1 limits. [KNOWN_ISSUES.md](KNOWN_ISSUES.md) lists bugs and untested ground. See [the roadmap](ROADMAP.md) for source, installed-package and measurement evidence.

## TIDE limits (reviewed 5 November 2026)

- **JRNY stays in the part's own engine.** It never changes engine (FM6 included). It picks within the starting sound's role family (pads and keys; bass; lead and pluck; hits; FX and textures; a USER record uses its own category) and rolls a new End B of the same sound when the family has no other, so a sound alone in its family does not move. It skips a part's last four destinations where it can; a small family (55 of the 66 engine and category groups still have four sounds or fewer) can repeat.
- **LEAD answers only on lead and pluck lines.** TURNS LEAD thins the follower part when its sound is a lead or a pluck playing a single-note line; a pad, a bass or a chord part keeps its pattern whole, and a pattern of two hits or fewer is left whole. TURNS has three values (TOGETHER, TAKE TURNS, LEAD); a stored ECHO reads as LEAD.
- **The parts coordinate timing and colour.** Attack prediction and parameter-based contrast do not analyse sustained harmony or effects tails. The automatic upper-voice movement works on ordinary chord sequences, not on ARP SEQ's held chord.
- **Unequal lengths are available; automatic polymeter is not.** Existing part lengths can rotate against a shared 4/4 reference. Automatic shortening/return policy and physical clock/listening acceptance remain open.
- **USER slots reuse the existing addresses; they add no capacity.** Voice slots 125 to 128 (U1 to U4) and pattern slots P29 to P32 show `USER EMPTY` until saved; the factory sounds and patterns they stand in for are read-only places after them. TIDE's store still has 21 chunks with six worst-case records each: 126 records, despite 128 addresses, so a USER slot on a full store can refuse a new save (STORE FULL). The 32 patterns share three byte-limited 11/11/10-slot objects. A record already in a USER address is kept and shown as USER.
- **Exact live-morph continuation is not promised.** After a restart, part 2's live End B was found changed. The fork owner does not require that state to continue exactly. Intentional saved records still need to retain their contents. TIDE preferences persist between normal boots and, from the next build, ride in the settings record (its reserved tail), so every backup holds them.
- **Program change reaches slots 1 to 128 only, and bank 1 is bank 0.** Program 124 to 127 selects USER 1 to USER 4 (empty slots load nothing; plain DUELL 1.1 loaded the factory sounds #125 to #128 there); the factory sounds those slots displaced and #129 to #163 cannot be called by program change. Load them on the unit, or save them into a USER slot first.
- **Going back from TIDE to plain DUELL 1.1 is not supported.** A TIDE patch whose End B is an FM6 MORPH TO target reads as its factory sound on plain DUELL. The slot is dropped for good at the next write of that chunk. Plain DUELL also zeroes TIDE's settings at its next settings save (they ride in the settings record's reserved tail), and TIDE then starts on DUB again. Patch chunk 21, where earlier TIDE builds kept the settings, stays out of TIDE's patch store (126 records). A record a stock unit kept there is never overwritten, and an earlier TIDE build still finds its own record there. Use the installer's return flow only on a unit whose patches you have backed up. See [RECOVERY.md](RECOVERY.md).
- **The FM6 voice bank is in the backup of a TIDE unit** (the editor's Backup page, the installer's backup and `jam_dev.py`): the 32 voices (SLOT B1 to B32) are saved and restored with the bank upload. A backup made by an older build holds none and leaves the unit's bank as it is. The bank stays on the unit through a flash and is wiped by ERASE DATA; keep the `.syx` file you loaded as well.
- **Expansion has a small measured program budget.** The development line (`jam11`, 5 November 2026) links to 576,204 B of the 581,564 B slot: about 5.4 KB free (RAM .data+.bss 68,432 of 73,728 B; pool 322,880 of 368,640 B). The stable build `187e9d0` left about 24.9 KB; the features since then used most of it, and the clean-up since the release candidate (dead code out, the TIDE core simplified) gave back some. Each release needs its own measurement. Software and emulator evidence does not establish physical audio, MIDI clock or CPU performance.

## Inherited DUELL 1.1 limits

- **Units tested.** DUELL has run on two FM-1 units, the author's, and more than 100 people have installed it through the Firmware Hub. Cables and hubs are untested.
- **The way back (V15)** has worked for the author. There is no known rollback beyond it. See [RECOVERY.md](RECOVERY.md).
- **Pattern slots share storage.** The 32 patterns are stored in groups: one storage object holds 11 slots in 3,584 bytes, so the 11 slots of a group share that room.
- **Hidden destinations.** LFO DEPTH and DEPTH 2 do not show as matrix destinations (the depth cards of the pages move them). Two factory patches, #28 TORCH SONG and #108 YO YA, still carry an aftertouch route to the hidden DEPTH 2, so their LFO 2 does nothing without aftertouch.
- **UNISON and DTUN have fixed reach.** UNISON DETUNE spreads the outer voices about 24 cents at its maximum, and the range is not widened. FM6's DTUN spreads the carriers up to 12 cents a step around the note (the outer ones 12 cents apart with two carriers, 60 with six); algorithms 17 to 19 have one carrier, so DTUN does nothing there. UNISON uses every voice the engine allows (up to 8; PHYS 3, VOICE 4), fewer when the NOTES budget needs the time.
- **KEY VELOCITY is on by default.** A very hard press can come out quiet; the feel has not been measured. You can switch it off in MENU > CONTROL. AFTERTOUCH is on by default too.
- **The editor has no MIDI MAP tab.** Assign CCs with MIDI LEARN on the unit.
- **SUB HARMONICS is switched off.** The HARM card is not on the SUB OSC page; CC17 has no effect.
- **No menu switch and no notice for the second core.** The fallback to one core is automatic and silent.
- **No error screen in the update loader.** The update screen shows progress only; if a write fails the installer page tells you.
- **MIDI** is not tested with many devices.
- **The audio clock is not exactly 44,100 Hz.** The output runs from a 24 MHz timer divided by 544: 44,117.6 Hz. The tempo, pitch and time constants assume 44,100 Hz. By that arithmetic the pitch is about 0.7 cent sharp and the internal tempo about 0.04 % fast (a bar of 2 s drifts 0.8 ms). The 24 MHz clock itself has not been measured on a unit. Nothing compensates for it.
- **Heavy multis** can miss an audio block very rarely (a short click). The NOTES budget limits the notes per part to avoid it; the CPU figures in the documents are a computer model, the unit can read higher.

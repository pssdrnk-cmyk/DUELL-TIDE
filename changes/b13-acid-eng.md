## changelog
### 1.0.0-b13
- **ACID can scream (owner 15 November: no squelch or scream by turning the ANALOG / ACID controls by hand).** Only on ANALOG WAVE ACID / ACSQ, every other sound bit for bit as before: RES goes on to the edge of self-oscillation as a 303's does (the resonant peak about +34 dB at RES 127, was +14: the old law stopped short, so RES 95 and RES 127 sounded nearly alike); SAT drives harder (to ~33x, was 9x) with its tone kept open (~7 kHz, was closing to 3 kHz, which darkened the resonance instead of screaming it): the 303-into-a-pedal scream; an accent's filter decay is Open303's (a time constant of 200 ms, the 303's shortest; was 200 ms to 99 %, a quarter of it), so accents go "wow" instead of ticking, with the accent push scaled so one accent opens the filter as far as before. Measured on a hand-turned line (CUT 30, ENV 2 24, DEC2 98): RES 127 + SAT 110 moves the centroid from 0.24 to ~1.9 kHz. Cost: 108 B flash, 88 B hot RAM (164 B flash with the knobs below), a few instructions a block (nothing per sample).
- **The 303's knobs on the ENGINE page** (owner: he could not squelch by hand): on WAVE ACID / ACSQ the ENGINE page reads WAVE, MULTI CUTOFF (ENV 2's depth, the ENV MOD) and DECAY (ENV 2's) in place of DETUNE and MIX; MIX moves to EDIT 1's free card. CUT and RES stay on the MULTI FILTER page. 56 B.
- The old M10 ACID TWO 303 and M11 ACID LINES play their 303 parts 3 .. 3.5 dB lower (the harder resonance made them louder than the multis' window); JOURNEY tables regenerated.

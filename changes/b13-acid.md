## changelog
### 1.0.0-b13
- **Acid made real (owner 15 November: the acid sounds and multis were "quite bad"):** three dedicated 303 voices on the ANALOG ACID / ACSQ waves, set to the TB-303's documented ranges (Open303, MIT, as ported in the x0x engine: cutoff 314 .. 2394 Hz, env mod 0.8 .. 4.6 octaves, filter decay 200 .. 2000 ms, accent = the shortest decay, flat amp, no key tracking): **#5 SAW 303** (was SQUELCH: the classic Chicago saw), **#6 SQUARE 303** (was TIDAL SUB: the deep, rubbery square), **#27 SCREAM 303** (was RAZORWIRE: resonance and env mod near the top, overdriven). No browse number moves. The old sounds' faults: SQUELCH was a plain SAW (the 303 path never ran in the sound itself), its filter decay 11 x shorter than a 303's shortest, key tracking and routes no 303 has; the multis opened the filter 6 .. 7 octaves on every note (a 303 reaches 4.6) and put chorus, spread and reverb on a mono 303.
- **M09 ACID HYPNO, M10 ACID TWO 303, M11 ACID LINES rebuilt around them:** each a 16-step line written by the 303 rules (root, octave, minor third, fourth, fifth, flat seventh, one chromatic neighbour; accents off the kick; slides into accents; an octave drop under a slide), gate 58 %, dry 303s into the dotted echo. M09: SQUARE 303 on A1, every kick left free, over the fog pad. M10: SCREAM 303 rolling under D2 off the kicks, accents just before them, SQUARE 303 answering an octave up. M11: SAW 303 Chicago line in E over the dark pad. Levels inside the multi window, no clipping, End B and TIDE travel checked by render.

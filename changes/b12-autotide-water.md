### 1.0.0-b12
- **Changed:** AUTO TIDE is the water page (owner's pick, "it's like a tide"). Knob 1's glide line, MODE, TIME and WAIT stay; under them each part's steps (taller, 6 to 32 px by pitch) stand in the sea, as high as that part's tide: low water at A, high water at B. The surface is a gentle swell (the splash sea's slow pace) whose crests catch the light; slack water (WAIT) rolls at half the pace in moonlight, armed holds still and dashed, stopped holds still and dim, a take is red. The water has three flat tones, darker lower down. Gone from the page, with their code: the SOUND / SEQ A-B rails and their titles, the part numbers and bar, each part's live line, the ARC progress line, the loop's bar cells, and the tide line under the header (other pages keep it). A moved or added step is drawn in its own part's colour lightened (it was the accent, part 1's sea glass, also in part 2's lane). The water rolls in the main loop only and sends only the rows that changed.

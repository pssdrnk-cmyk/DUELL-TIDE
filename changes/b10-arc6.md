## changelog
### 1.0.0-b10
- **Changed (ARC you can hear):** ARC now plays through the sound as well as the notes, on PERFORM's FILTER while K3 is at rest (no new setting). The rise starts muffled and opens bar by bar (a low-pass from about 400 Hz; the first arc after PLAY from about 2.5 kHz), its last 2 bars pull the low end out like a riser, the peak lands with the full low end on its first beat, and the breakdown keeps its notes without their weight (a high-pass near 140 Hz). Measured on 7 multis over a whole arc: the peak is 0.5 to 1.6 octaves brighter than the start of the rise (was 0 to 0.3, sometimes darker), and the breakdown's low end is below the peak's on 6 of 7 (was at or above it on 5). Turning K3 plays your filter instead. Drone multis keep ARC off (STYLE DRONE).

## changelog
### 1.0.0-b12
- **Added:** M27 EMPTY, a blank slate for playing live. It sits after M26 in SAVE > MULTIS as a group of its own, and knobs 1 and 4 now wrap, so one step left of M01 (or right of M26) lands on it; MIDI program change 27 loads it too. Part 1 is FM6 TINE ROOM, part 2 ANALOG SQUARE LEAD (release shortened), LAYER, 120 BPM, no steps, arp OFF, sends 0, no key. A load starts both parts at A with AUTO TIDE idle, as any multi does. It is a template in ROM with no flash object (M01-M26 and the flash layout are unchanged): SAVE on it moves the pick to the multi that was loaded before it (M01 at power-on) with "PICK A SLOT, SAVE" and the normal save goes on from there; ERASE says ALREADY FACTORY. 220 B of flash on the device build (record 306 B packed, `tools/drone_pack.py` rom 27).

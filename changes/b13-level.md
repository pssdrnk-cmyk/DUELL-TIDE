## changelog
### 1.0.0-b13
- **Output level +8 dB:** the jacks and headphones get 8 dB more at every MASTER position (owner: "very quiet compared to Shrub and my other synths"). All of it comes after the limiter, so the limiter works as before and the multis keep their balance (each within 0.03 dB of its old place). The DAC stage uses its full scale: `audio.c` `OUT_SHIFT` 7 -> 8 (+6 dB; the old -6 dBFS ceiling came from the first bring-up), plus a fixed +2 dB on MASTER's target. The master's soft knee now tops out at -3.5 dBFS (`fx.c` `MK_TOP`), which puts the loudest sample at -1.5 dBFS at the DAC. Measured at full MASTER: the factory multis -18.8 LUFS median (were -26.8), true peak at most -1.0 dBFS; 8-note chords on the hottest pads and 8-note chords over the busiest multis, 0 clipped samples. USB audio is unchanged. The knee touches at most 0.03 % of a multi's samples. Flash +4 B, no CPU (three shifts a block). Report: `work/b13/level/REPORT.md` (workspace).

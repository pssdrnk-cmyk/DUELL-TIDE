## changelog
### 1.0.0-b10
- **Changed:** MENU > COLOR has four themes: TIDE (first, the default), PAPER (light), GREY and MONO. VIOLET, ORANGE, MINT, COBALT, PALE, OCEAN and POLICE are gone (-528 B of flash). A unit that had GREY, MONO or TIDE keeps it; a unit that had a removed theme starts in TIDE (an old backup with a removed theme restores to TIDE too). The web editor's colour list follows the unit.

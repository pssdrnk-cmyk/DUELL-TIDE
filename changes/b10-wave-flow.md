## changelog
### 1.0.0-b10
- **Changed (the owner on rc6.1):** the wave page flows with the tempo: the stack moves back one line a 16th note, smoothly (a tempo change or a MIDI clock changes its speed, never jumps it), playing or stopped; older lines settle flatter as they move back, and flat and settled lines carry slow, very small ripples (under a pixel, a round a bar) that ride back with them. A stab's shape now flows away instead of snapping to flat lines.

## changelog
### 1.0.0-b10
- **Changed (flash room, b11 tests lane):** FM6's exp2 table `FM6_EXP2` has 257 entries instead of 1025 (generated by `tools/gen_tables.py`; `fm6_mant` indexes with `f >> 16` and interpolates over the low 16 bits, nothing else changed): 3,072 B of flash (device build 580,056 B -> 576,984 B, free 1,508 B -> 4,580 B). Worst relative error of FM6's exp2 against double precision 9.2e-7 (was 5.8e-8), i.e. at most 0.0016 cents of pitch; new group `fm6exp2` (`tests/fm6_exp2_test.c`) checks it over the whole input range. It is a sound change in the bits: the 27 FM6 renders of `regress` and 27 of `mrender` are re-made (`golden.txt`, `multi_render_golden.txt`, `jam/repin.sh`, proven by revert), `jrny_mu.h` regenerated (M10 part 2 level step 41 -> 40); the level of every render moves by at most 0.007 dB.

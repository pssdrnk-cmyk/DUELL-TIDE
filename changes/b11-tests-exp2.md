## changelog
### 1.0.0-b10
- **Changed (flash room, b11 tests lane):** FM6's exp2 table `FM6_EXP2` has 513 entries instead of 1025 (generated by `tools/gen_tables.py`; `fm6_mant` indexes with `f >> 15` and interpolates over the low 15 bits, nothing else changed): 2,052 B of flash (device build 580,056 B -> 578,004 B, free 1,508 B -> 3,560 B). Worst relative error of FM6's exp2 against double precision 2.3e-7 (was 5.8e-8), i.e. at most 0.0004 cents of pitch; new group `fm6exp2` (`tests/fm6_exp2_test.c`) checks it over the whole input range. It is a change in the bits: 53 FM6 renders drift apart in phase (waveforms differ, the long-term third-octave spectrum moves by at most 0.24 dB in any band, the level by at most 0.002 dB); `golden.txt` and `multi_render_golden.txt` re-made (`jam/repin.sh`, proven by revert).

### 1.0.0-b10
- **New:** every multi position has a flash slot of its own. M17 to M26 (acid, electro, ambient and the two drones) can be saved over, renamed and erased like M01 to M16; ERASE brings the built-in multi back. The storage holds exactly M26 (five new objects at 0xEA000..0xF3FFF); a unit from before reads them as empty, nothing is rewritten. Backups carry them (ids 42 to 46, caps 127, archive version 6); version 5 backups load as before and leave M17 and up alone.

## changelog
### 1.0.0-b12
- **Every sound editable:** every one of the 171 factory sounds has its own slot now (175 slots: #1 to #124, USER 1 to USER 4, #125 to #171). Save over any of them; Restore gives the factory sound back. The unit keeps up to 150 of your own versions at once: 126 among the first 128 slots, as before, and 24 among #125 to #171 (past that it says STORE FULL). Going back to an earlier build hides your versions of #125 to #171 but does not lose them. Program change bank 2, programs 124 to 127, now reach #125 to #128. The web editor lists and writes all 175 slots, and its backup (version 7) carries them; older backups still restore. Nothing you saved moves.

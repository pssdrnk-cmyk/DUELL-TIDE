## changelog
### 1.0.0-b10
- **Changed (levels, b11):** eight factory sounds that read 6 to 9 LU louder than their role when chorded are lowered as far as the sound audit allows (the owner's "quiet body" rule: a held note under -21 dBFS fails): TRIM DARK MIN7 112 -> 109, SINGING BOWL 124 -> 120, LOW STAB 110 -> 105, MOTOR VIBES 120 -> 117, PLATE CHOIR 95 -> 94 and its REV send 114 -> 40 (the chord case 2.1 LU quieter), HORNET 96 -> 91, GEIGER 102 -> 97, SOFT EP 91 -> 86. Worst case (note or chord, End A or B) over the role median: 9.3 -> 7.8, 8.2 -> 6.2, 7.5 -> 5.0, 7.2 -> 5.7, 7.0 -> 5.1, 6.8 -> 4.3, 6.7 -> 4.2, 6.1 -> 3.6 LU. The factory multis that load LOW STAB (M07), DARK MIN7 (M20) and SOFT EP (M21) keep their level: each part now sets its own TRIM (110 / 112 / 91), so those records are byte for byte as before. M21 HSE LOFI: part 2 (FM BASS 2) LEVEL 107 -> 88 and part 1 LEVEL 104 -> 108, so a held chord no longer makes it 7 LU louder than the rest (chord held -13.3 -> -18.0 LUFS, no keys -20.72 -> -20.75).

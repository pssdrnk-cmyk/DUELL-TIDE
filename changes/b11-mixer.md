## changelog
### 1.0.0-b10
- **Changed (MIXER and QUICK GLO, design pass):** the mute hint is one row of chips, "MUTE 1 [GLO][OCT-]", instead of a sentence; under it each part shows its LOW, MID and HIGH as three bands (HIGH on top) in the part's colour, on the LEVEL fader's dB scale, each with a peak-hold tick. They follow the part's real sound and are worked out only while the page is shown, outside the audio interrupt.

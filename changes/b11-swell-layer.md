## changelog
### 1.0.0-b10
- **Fixed (SWELL / RIPPLE on the quick sequence layer):** with SEQ held, the layer showed no sequence and the press hardly registered, so the new notes were not seen. The layer now draws the selected part's pattern as bars next to G4 KEEP (what SWELL, RIPPLE and the other tools wrote shows at once, also when it lands at the loop start or a stopped transport applies it), its redraw signature includes the steps and a waiting press, the SWELL or RIPPLE box that waits for the loop start is lit, and the header line "SWELL  NEXT LOOP" stays until it lands, however long the loop.

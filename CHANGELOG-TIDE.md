# Changelog of DUELL with TIDE

What changed in the fork (pssdrnk's DUELL-TIDE). Upstream's own history is in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md); the notes of DUELL 1.1 are in [RELEASE_NOTES.md](RELEASE_NOTES.md).

New lines for the fork go here, not into CHANGELOG.md.

Not an OSCILL/R release: pssdrnk's fork of DUELL 1.1 (<https://github.com/pssdrnk-cmyk/DUELL-TIDE>), adding TIDE, a morph that plays and evolves by itself. TIDE has its own version, `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`, kept in the file `TIDE_VERSION` and shown in MENU > SYSTEM > ABOUT beside the DUELL version (1.1). A build flashed for testing is a beta, `1.0.0-b1`, `1.0.0-b2`, ...; the first build the fork owner has validated on the unit is `1.0.0`; a fix is `1.0.1`, a feature `1.1.0`. The git tag is `tide-` and the version (`tide-1.0.0-b1`); the GitHub release has the same name. Each version has a section below, `## 1.0.0-b1`, newest first; a section heading names the tag and, once known, the commit and the date it was flashed. The commit stays in the release notes, not in the name. The installer page offers the stable and the beta build of the [GitHub releases](https://github.com/pssdrnk-cmyk/DUELL-TIDE/releases); the [README](README.md#builds-stable-and-beta) says which they are.

## 1.0.0-b9 (in progress)

- **Changed (the delay's echo, every multi):** the repeats now leave through the delay's COLR filter from the very first one (before, only the copy fed back was darkened, so the first repeat came out as bright as the note), and the low cut inside the echo loop moves from about 165 Hz to about 219 Hz so the repeats keep less low mud. Every echo is a little darker and cleaner; PING-PONG's left repeat and the RANDOM / OCT UP modes are unchanged. Nothing to set.
- **Changed (M01 BASIC CHORD and M02 DUB CHORD get real dub space and weight):** the echo hangs on longer (feedback 110, each repeat duller: COLR 26, a wobbling delay time), M02 gets a real plate wash behind the stab, and under both stabs a low held minor-7 bed now plays with its root an octave down (G1 and C2), where there was a noise bed (M01) and a thin pad (M02). M01's stab goes through tape. The crackle bed of M01 is gone.
- **Changed (M03 to M08, house and Detroit):** JUNO DEEP's pad no longer rubs in semitone clusters and has a wider chorus; LATE RHODES, ORGAN HOUSE, PIANO HOUSE, DETROIT STAB and HI-TECH SOUL get more width and echo, ORGAN HOUSE's riff no longer clashes with its pad, HI-TECH SOUL's bell is back in the chord. LATE RHODES' low shelf is out (the Rhodes keeps its bass).
- **Changed (M09 to M16, acid, electro, IDM, Berlin, ambient):** longer, wider echoes and bigger reverbs on all eight; HYPNO 303 sits on a minor-7 fog pad; TWO 303S spreads wider with a hall behind it and still no pad; ACID LINES' pad is darker and the line gets a fixed note; DEEP SEA ARP's pad plays written chords inside the arpeggio's own F sharp minor notes (no more clashes); WARPED TAPE and BERLIN RUN get a darker, longer echo; TWO LOOPS' bells are louder and wetter.
- **Changed (the drones get a low end):** the two drone multis (M17 DUNE HORN, M18 VOID DEEP) now have a soft sub an octave under their notes, and the low cuts on the drone sounds (HORN SWELL, RAIN STRINGS, SOLAR WIND) are gone. A quarter to a third of their sound now sits under 150 Hz (before: none). VOID SHIMMER is a little louder.

## 1.0.0-b8 (in progress)

A polish beta on top of b7: small fixes, leftovers out, tools and docs made true.

- **Changed (HOME page 1's knobs):** the MORPH page's knobs 2 to 4 are now FLT, RSN and ECH: knob 3 is the filter's resonance on every engine, knob 4 the delay send; LENGTH is gone from the page (it stays on the ENV page). The row reads FLT, RSN, ECH; OCT- plus a knob still puts the loaded sound's value back, and BOTH moves each part by the same steps.
- **New (ACID):** the ANALOG engine's WAVE list ends with ACID (a saw) and ACSQ (a square). A sound with either plays its accents like a TB-303: an accented note shortens the filter sweep to about 200 ms whatever the DECAY says, adds a short burst of level (about +9 dB at its start, falling back onto the note's level in 200 ms) that follows the sweep, and pushes the cutoff up through a lag that grows with the RESONANCE, so accents in a row climb higher, more at high RESONANCE. An ACID note plays at one fixed level whatever its velocity (about 7 dB under a SAW note at the same TRIM; the accent reaches the full level), and a slide is the 303's soft RC lag (90 % in 53 ms, 99 % in 106 ms). Every other sound is unchanged. Slides stay the pattern's (SLIDE steps, a LEGATO sound).
- **Changed (M09 is now HYPNO 303):** a hypnotic acid-techno line on the square 303 (ACSQ), fifteen steps against the bar so it slowly shifts, its filter opening as the ride goes, over the dark fog pad, 128 BPM. It replaces MINIMAL 12.
- **Changed (M11 ACID LINES):** now a real acid-house line: a 303-style saw in E minor at 122 BPM with accents and slides, the filter snapping shut on each plain note, and a slow pad under it feeding a long echo.
- **Changed (M10 is now TWO 303S):** acid techno in D minor at 130 BPM, the Hardfloor / Josh Wink way: two distorted 303s and no pad, a saw line hard left against a square line of thirteen steps hard right, answering in its rests, into a long dotted echo. It replaces SEVEN LOOP.
- SAVE after JOURNEY says what it saves: when the part plays a sound JOURNEY (or MORPH TO) moved it to, the SAVE dialog shows "SAVES THE LOADED SOUND / KEEP FIRST = AS HEARD" above its question. What SAVE stores did not change; do KEEP first to save what you hear.
- ARP SEQ parts stay on the beat on the internal clock: on a long set (400 bars) the arp used to run up to a tenth of a second ahead of the bars and the click, at 117 BPM about 12 samples a bar; now it is exact, at any tempo and after a tap tempo.
- **Under the hood (b8 ROOM):** HOME's old MODE page code was already out of TIDE's build; the last piece that only that page used (a KNOB 4 check in the output-mode knob) is out too, 4 bytes. Every TIDE screen is pixel-identical (923 renders compared). The factory multis tool rebuilds all eighteen records byte for byte (checked), so the patch step for the End B amounts is not needed.
- **Fixed (ARC):** the first beat of a new ARC phase plays with the new phase's settings. The breakdown's first beat is thinned out and the peak's first loop gets no POLY stretch, as the rest of the phase does; before, the old phase's settings ran for the first beat or two.
- **Under the hood (test):** the step-display test `tidesteps` plays factory multi 9 (MINIMAL 12) again, also over several ARC DROP arcs, and finds no step shown that does not play. Its two readers of the ARP SEQ step (`ui_draw.c`, `seq_dialogue.c`) were checked: both already follow a POLY stretch.
- **Under the hood (ARC):** the dead TURNS leftovers are gone: the TURNS variable, ARC's late-rise flag (nothing read either since CALL AND ANSWER became always on) and TURNS' column in the STYLE table. The saved TIDE settings keep their size and every other field as they were; the TURNS byte is always written as 1 and never read back. Nothing you hear or see changes.

## 1.0.0-b7 (in progress)

Built overnight on 9-10 November 2026 from the musical review (owner: "your ideas look good, go ahead with all of them", "let's keep what makes musical sense"). Not yet on the FM-1.

### What TIDE plays

- **Changed (ARC):** ARC moves on 8-bar boundaries. Every phase starts on bar 1 of an 8, the peak lasts 8 or 16 bars, and one arc is 40 to 64 bars. ARC DROP drops on the first arc after PLAY (about 40 seconds in), and the shape of the arcs differs from night to night. A drop's two silent bars are silent from their first downbeat, and ARC OFF ends a drop at once.
- **Changed (ARC):** the breakdown is a real breakdown. Both parts keep only their main hits, chords drop to two voices and the level falls by 2 to 5 dB. The parts thin out by voices and level; the number of hits falls less (on M02 the breakdown still has about nine tenths of the rise's hits, by voices it is about two thirds).
- **Changed (ARP SEQ parts):** the factory chord parts follow TIDE's dynamics now: ARC's peak is louder and its breakdown softer (M02: peak 112, breakdown 62 in MIDI velocity; it was flat at 100). An accented step keeps its accent.
- **New (call and answer):** in WANDER and JOURNEY the two parts take turns bar by bar. One plays out while the other steps back to its main hits (chords in two voices, a little softer); both play the fourth bar. A bass or pad part is never pushed back. TURNS plays nothing now; the answer is built in.
- **Changed (the bass):** a bass part keeps its loop length and its octave. TIDE no longer shortens its loop (POLY) or moves its notes an octave (VOIC), and a shortened loop ends when ARC's breakdown begins. M02's bass keeps its pickup.
- **Changed (motifs):** a figure of five to seven hits plays whole most of the time (M09 DEEP 332's stab plays whole in 72 % of the bars, before 18 %). A figure with only one free hit keeps every hit outside the breakdown.
- **New (chord part):** the chord part breathes with ARC. Its voicing opens at the peak and closes, lower and tighter, in the breakdown. Same notes, other octaves.
- **New (chord part):** the variation bar of a chord part plays a rhythm a house or dub-techno player knows (the skank with a throw, the Detroit two-hit, 3-3-2 and more), the one nearest its own pattern, with the pattern's own chords. M02 plays the Detroit two-hit, M05 and M08 the skank with a throw, M09 3-3-2 in 16ths.
- **Fixed (VOIC):** with OFF-SCALE PLAY, VOIC keeps a chord's out-of-scale note (a Bb in C major stays a Bb).
- **New (hand on the fader):** grab the MORPH or SEQ fader while TIDE runs and TIDE waits for you with that part only. The other part, the pattern's own movement and ARC keep going. 8 bars after you let go, at the next phrase, TIDE takes the part back from where you left it. AUTO TIDE says HAND  BACK IN n BARS.
- **Changed (JOURNEY):** the dip between two sounds on a long trip lasts a few bars (closing over 2, closed for 1, opening over 2) instead of most of the trip; FM6 sounds keep their sparkle for longer. The sound between the two ends is still duller than either end on some multis (not solved).
- **Changed (JOURNEY):** it keeps your multi's mix. A part's mixer level never changes on the way, and a part the multi turned down stays down on the sounds JOURNEY travels to. M05 ORGAN SKANK, M06, M08 TINE DUB and M16 FINAL REEL were 6 to 7 dB too loud on a JOURNEY; now M05 and M06 are within 2 dB, M16 is on average 1 dB over (some bars more) and M08 sits 3 dB under its own sounds. The colour of the sounds on the way is not changed yet.
- **Fixed (JOURNEY):** started with the fader away from A, JOURNEY no longer swaps the sound you are hearing; it travels to the B that is there first.
- **Changed (AUTO TIDE):** multis that wait for keys play on their own under AUTO TIDE: any pad, HOLD ALL part or arp you made sounds a chord as soon as AUTO TIDE runs. Press a key and the chord is yours until the next PLAY.

### The MORPH page

- **Changed (MORPH, owner: page 1 is the hands-on page):** knob 1 MORPH takes half the turning (two steps a click; A and B still exact), knob 2 is FILTER (the cutoff, as QUICK EDIT's knob 1), knob 3 ECHO (the delay send; dim, with the reason on knob 1's line, when SENDS are OFF or the delay's MIX is 0), knob 4 LENGTH (the release). The values sit on the slim row under the wave picture. They are the sound's own settings: they record, take MIDI LEARN, save, and don't hold TIDE's glide. TIDE still takes the fader back 8 bars after your hand lets go.
- **Changed (the MORPH page's row, owner on the unit):** the row under the picture reads FLT, ECHO and LEN, each name in a fixed place with its value beside it, so even the widest values (10.1k, 100%, 104ms) never crowd the next one.
- **Changed (BOTH):** with ALGORITHM on BOTH every knob of the MORPH page moves each part from its own value by the same steps, the fader too; before, the other part jumped to the selected part's fader and AMOUNT.
- **Changed (rolls):** a new End B is OCT+ held and a click of knob 1 (the octave stays). The new-A roll is gone from the page (MIDI's ROLL A still works). OCT- and a knob: knob 1 back to your original sound, knobs 2 to 4 back to the loaded sound's value.
- **Moved (AMOUNT):** AMOUNT is a TIDE SETTINGS row, after ROLL TYPE, with your stored value. It is no longer dimmed in JOURNEY, where it still shapes a roll.

### The settings page

- **New (owner, 9 and 10 November: "the selection is actively deciding the play mode", "1-layer-2 when changed in the menu then 1-stereo-2"):** on MORPH, TIDE SETTINGS, AUTO TIDE and in PERFORM, ALGORITHM has three positions: PART 1, the middle, PART 2. The middle selects both parts (the knobs, the fader and AUTO TIDE act on both, each part from its own value, as BOTH did) and plays the mode of the PLAY MODE row, LAYER, STEREO or DUEL; the header then reads that word. PART 1 and PART 2 play that part alone on every key, whatever PLAY MODE says (LAYER no longer plays both parts there). With SPLIT set, the middle splits the keys in STEREO and DUEL too (below the split part 1, from it part 2). Changing the PLAY MODE row moves ALGORITHM to the middle, so you hear it at once; a multi saved in LAYER loads in the middle. **With PLAY MODE on LAYER, moving between a side and the middle lets go of held notes, so a held chord stops** (a short dip). Nothing new is stored. The edit pages still pick PART 1 / PART 2.
- **New (owner, 9 November: "a new split mode: middle"):** SPLIT MIDDLE, the value after OFF on the SPLIT row. The keys split in the middle wherever OCT- / OCT+ put them: the 13 lower keys play part 1, the 14th (F#4 at octave 0) and up part 2; MIDI notes split at the note that key plays now. Out of OFF the first turn right is MIDDLE, the next the note it stands for. A learned SPLIT CC: 0 OFF, 1 MIDDLE, 2 to 127 the note. Kept in the settings and multis, no format change (a b6 build reads MIDDLE as OFF).
- **Changed (flash):** the factory pattern table keeps its flags packed (the same notes and flags, 640 B smaller), to make room for the two above.
- **Changed (TIDE SETTINGS, owner: "let's keep what makes musical sense"):** the page has ten rows: STYLE, MODE, TARGET, ARC, ROLL TYPE, AMOUNT (from the MORPH page), then REC, PLAY MODE, XFADE and SPLIT. VARY BARS, TURNS, POLY, OFFBEAT and WASH have no rows; each STYLE sets them. ROLL ARP and ROLL PITCH have no row either (OFF; a unit that had one ON keeps it, and a learned MIDI CC can still switch it). MODE is the same setting as K2 on AUTO TIDE. Your saved STYLE stays that style after the update.
- **Changed (STYLE DUB):** DUB plays WANDER: the multi's own sounds stay and evolve instead of travelling to others, so the level and brightness stay steady (M02: brightness swing 2x, not 7x; dulled bars 7 %, not 51 %). A DUB you saved with b6 reads as DUB with WANDER. The first start is still M02 DUB CHORD.
- **Changed (STYLE AMBIENT):** AMBIENT no longer shortens loops (POLY OFF).
- **Changed (ROLL TYPE):** ROLL TYPE no longer switches the rhythm off; ARC and the pattern's movement act with any ROLL TYPE. ROLL TYPE is dim under JOURNEY, where it does nothing (knob 1's line says NOT JRNY).

### The multis

- **Changed (the reel re-voiced, after hearing it on the unit):** every multi now holds a chord or pad through the whole bar under its front part, rings into a long dotted echo, and has chorus and width on the front sound; the voices were chosen from the instruments of the genre's reference records (Juno pad, DX-type electric piano, M1-style organ, 303-style acid line with a pad under it, Moog-style sequence, FM bells). Four parts were swapped for held ones (ORGAN HOUSE, MINIMAL 12, ACID LINES, ROBOT FUNK). M02 DUB CHORD keeps its FM stab and long echo and now holds C minor under it instead of a sparse sub line, so it no longer drops out.
- **New (the reel: fifteen new factory multis, one curated programme):** the set runs like a night, a genre each: M01 BASIC CHORD (dub techno), M02 DUB CHORD (dub techno, rebuilt over a held pad), M03 JUNO DEEP and M04 LATE RHODES (deep house), M05 ORGAN HOUSE and M06 PIANO HOUSE (classic house), M07 DETROIT STAB and M08 HI-TECH SOUL (Detroit), M09 MINIMAL 12 (minimal), M10 SEVEN LOOP (hypnotic techno), M11 ACID LINES (acid), M12 ROBOT FUNK and M13 DEEP SEA ARP (electro), M14 WARPED TAPE (IDM), M15 BERLIN RUN (Berlin school), M16 TWO LOOPS (ambient). Each was researched from how its records are made, its two voices set for their roles and its patterns written as phrases. Every one plays both parts on PLAY with no key held. A held key moves a multi by its distance from C (hold C: as written). Three are written note by note and stay in their key: JUNO DEEP (A minor), PIANO HOUSE (C minor) and ACID LINES (A minor, with real slides and accents). JUNO DEEP, ORGAN HOUSE and BERLIN RUN carry a bass-register line (the genre's own): with a bass synth playing, mute one of the two. The FM-1 has no sampled piano, so PIANO HOUSE is an FM electric grand. End B of every part is set by hand (the acid lines open, the Berlin sequence opens, the tape pad wobbles more). They replace last night's six genre scenes, ORGAN SKANK, WARM ECHO, TINE DUB, DEEP 332 and the owner's PAD SPLIT, WHISPER TIDE, TINE STRINGS and FINAL REEL (all in the git history). The set's levels sit within 2 dB of each other playing alone.
- **Changed (beds):** BASIC CHORD's crackle bed and the shimmer beds of DUNE HORN and VOID DEEP sound on PLAY with no key held. DUNE HORN and VOID DEEP move half as fast per step on the internal clock (as designed under a 120 BPM clock) and are 5 dB louder.

### Fixes

- **Fixed (review FR1):** a swung pattern (SWG over 50, the part's or TEMPO SWING; DEEP SHUFFLE, HOUSE STAB, DEEP 332, DOT DELAY) stays on the grid when TIDE's POLY plays it 15 or 13 steps long. Before, it came back from the stretch late against the other part, the click and an external kick (GENTLE 15 at SWG 60 by about a sixteenth and a half, WIDE by less) and stayed late until STOP / PLAY; during the stretch every other pass was off the grid too. - **Fixed (review FR7):** OCT- held with a PRESETS turn or a knob no longer also acts when you let go: no octave drop on AUTO TIDE or in PERFORM, no jump HOME from PATTERNS or MULTIS, no step back of the row on TIDE SETTINGS.

- **Fixed (review FR7):** SEQ + REC arms recording however you let go of the two buttons (together, or SEQ first); before, REC flipped HOLD.
- **Fixed (review FR7):** cancelling a dialog with HOME or a page button says NOT SAVED only when something was to be saved (SAVE, NAME); INITIALIZE SOUND, CLEAR and RESTORE ALL say CANCELLED.
- **Fixed (review FR6):** loading one of your saved sounds, multis or patterns while you play no longer cuts notes the other part is holding (the second core is no longer parked for a store read). Not yet checked on the unit.
- **Fixed (review FR10):** the load UNDO (OCT- held + PRESETS) and AUTO TIDE's HOME bring your saved sound back with its own End B and the values you set by hand, not a new random End B.

- **Fixed (review FR11):** ERASE DATA's dialog names everything it erases, the user wave tables and the FM6 voice bank included; INSTALL and QUICKSTART say the same.

## 1.0.0-b6

Tag `tide-1.0.0-b6` (jam11 after the b6 PR), flashed 9 November 2026. 1.0.0-b6 also carries every change listed under [1.0.0-b5](#100-b5): b5 was never published on its own (owner, 9 November: "let's move to b6"), so its items ship here. Measured on the unit for b5 (16-multi GLIDE sweep, test-mode build, MORPH on screen): no missed audio blocks and no voices cut, worst block 83 % (M05). b6 keeps b5's note counts (the tried step is below), so that measurement stands; the drones M17 and M18 were measured on the unit too (no missed blocks, no voices cut).

- **Changed (MORPH page, owner 2026-11-09: "the front lines move a lot and the ones behind barely", "check why the current display doesn't move when piece is playing", "the initial version looked quite cool when we called it wave lab"):** the waves as a WAVE LAB stack: 13 lines a part scrolling back every picture, each as tall as the front, fading toward the back; nothing stands still while a part sounds; an older line never crosses a newer one. In detail: the waves are drawn as WAVE LAB's WAVE STACK at b5's size: 13 lines a part, 2 rows apart, each as tall as the front and coloured from the part's colour at the front toward the background at the back. Every picture becomes the front, about 16 a second, and the whole stack moves back a step, so every line moves about as much as the front. Before, the lines behind lay flat when the sound's shape changed or a new note started (with #91 DRAWBARS in a sequence, about 9 frames in 10), and a part's picture could stand still for a quarter second while it played (DUB CHORD). Each new picture is blended half and half into the front while the two are of a size, so a busy sound flows instead of tangling. An older line never crosses a newer one, as with a saw on changing notes (about 1 picture in 15 before): where it would cross, it runs just above the newer line and keeps its shape elsewhere. The separate drawing for sounds with no steady period (pads, SLOW TIDE) is gone, as the blend does its work. Idle stays all flat; the layout and the ROLL / AMOUNT row are unchanged.
- **Changed (MORPH page, owner on the FM-1: "the new UI is definitely laggy"):** the MORPH page redraws in under half the time it took, and faster than b5's with its six lines (measured in the emulator build): the 12 lines behind the front are plain 1-px lines, as WAVE LAB drew them, instead of anti-aliased strokes, so they show small pixel steps; the front keeps its smooth 2 px stroke. The motion, the colours and the no-crossing rule are unchanged.
- **Fixed (MORPH page, owner on the FM-1: "the UI gets weird if I use morph during the animation"):** turning MORPH (knob 1) while the waves play no longer slows the waves to a crawl. Every fader value redrew both wave halves, and every 8th the whole page, so the screen's link was busy for most of each frame and the waves came at about a third of their pace (5 to 6 pictures a second instead of 11, worse on a quick turn). The colours of the page and of the waves now follow the fader on the frame that brings a new picture, in the steps the page's colours already used: the waves keep about 9 pictures a second while you turn (estimated from the pixels each frame sends), and the colours follow the fader within a picture (60 ms).
- **Changed (MORPH page, owner 2026-11-09: "something is missing"):** the waves have depth. The stack recedes like a landscape: each older line is a little narrower and centred (the oldest at 4/5 of the width) and the lines close up toward the back; each line hides what lies behind it, as in WAVE LAB; the lines now reach the screen's right edge. The motion, the colours, the no-crossing rule and the drawing time are as before (measured in the emulator build).
- **Fixed (MORPH page, the owner on the FM-1: "it's choppy", "somehow the smoothness is off"):** the waves move at an even pace. A new picture now comes 60 ms after the last one, by the clock, instead of on every 4th pass of the main loop, whose passes take from a few ms to over 40 (a pass that redraws the waves is long), so the pictures came at uneven gaps. The older lines are smooth again: each is drawn as a 1-pixel anti-aliased stroke from its height in 1/16 of a pixel, so they no longer show small steps, at about the cost of the plain lines.
- **Changed (the owner on the FM-1: "the animation feels laggy again"):** the screen link at 20 MHz, from 12: every page redraws faster (a full wave picture about 26 ms instead of 44, worked out from the clock). On the unit, MORPH with M05 playing: the main loop 40 passes a second instead of 36; the audio is unchanged. 30 MHz was tried and was no smoother; the panel is rated to about 62 MHz.
- **Changed (MENU > COLOR, owner 2026-11-09: "especially when changing the color scheme"):** turning through the colour themes no longer queues a full repaint for every step: the screen takes the new theme at most every 100 ms (each repaint is the whole screen, about 77 ms over the display's link), the first step at once and the last one always. Under the hood: test-mode builds can set the display's SPI clock (`DUELL_LCD_BAUD`) for a speed trial on the unit.
- **Changed (MORPH page, owner 2026-11-09):** at rest the page shows the same stack as when it plays, every line flat, at the same heights and widths (owner: "there is a fuckup with the default screen"; the rest screen had its own even spread, with the front line apart from the others). The lines' spacing no longer jumps (it went 3, 2, 3 pixels from the front): the gaps are whole rows that never widen further back, at least 2 rows apart.

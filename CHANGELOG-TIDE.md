# Changelog of DUELL with TIDE

What changed in the fork (pssdrnk's DUELL-TIDE). Upstream's own history is in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md); the notes of DUELL 1.1 are in [RELEASE_NOTES.md](RELEASE_NOTES.md).

New lines for the fork go here, not into CHANGELOG.md.

Not an OSCILL/R release: pssdrnk's fork of DUELL 1.1 (<https://github.com/pssdrnk-cmyk/DUELL-TIDE>), adding TIDE, a morph that plays and evolves by itself. TIDE has its own version, `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`, kept in the file `TIDE_VERSION` and shown in MENU > SYSTEM > ABOUT beside the DUELL version (1.1). A build flashed for testing is a beta, `1.0.0-b1`, `1.0.0-b2`, ...; the first build the fork owner has validated on the unit is `1.0.0`; a fix is `1.0.1`, a feature `1.1.0`. The git tag is `tide-` and the version (`tide-1.0.0-b1`); the GitHub release has the same name. Each version has a section below, `## 1.0.0-b1`, newest first; a section heading names the tag and, once known, the commit and the date it was flashed. The commit stays in the release notes, not in the name. The installer page offers the stable and the beta build of the [GitHub releases](https://github.com/pssdrnk-cmyk/DUELL-TIDE/releases); the [README](README.md#builds-stable-and-beta) says which they are.

## 1.0.0-b5

Not flashed yet.

- **Removed (owner, 2026-11-08: RAM for the planned wave picture on the MORPH page):** MORPH UNDO. A roll (by hand, by MIDI, or TIDE's own WNDR and JRNY rolls) now replaces its end; the morph keeps no history of earlier rolls. Knob 3 on the MORPH page is an empty card and does nothing for now. The UNDO target is gone from MIDI LEARN and typed MIDI lines: its code stays reserved, so every other CC and the shipped map are unchanged, and a line on UNDO saved by an earlier build is dropped when the settings load. Saved patches, multis, the project, the settings and backups never held the history, so they load as before. Also with `DUELL_TIDE=0`. About 1.9 KB of RAM freed (1,888 B) and about 1 KB of flash.

## 1.0.0-b4

Not flashed yet.

- **New (owner, 2026-11-08):** STYLE DRONE, after PEAK, for the eight factory drone sounds (#164 to #171) played as a held chord (HOLD latches the notes): WNDR, TIME 64, WAIT OFF, VARY EVERY 4TH, TURNS TOGETHER, POLY OFF, SYNC OFF, ARC OFF, WASH HIGH. A saved STYLE loads as before (CUSTOM is now 6; an old CUSTOM stored as 5 still loads as CUSTOM). About 4 B of flash. A file pack of eight drone multis, one per drone, is in `packs/drones/`.

- **Changed (owner, 2026-11-08: room in flash for the wave picture):** the SEQUENCER HELP page is gone, so a tap of SEQ goes STEP, then PARAMETER LOCKS. The MORPH page shows its fader value (A, 1 to 126, B) in knob 1's card like every other page, and its moving lines are gone; the top of the page keeps a quiet line until the planned wave picture takes that place. The four knobs, ALGORITHM, Hold SAVE and the OCT- shortcuts work as before. About 4.9 KB of flash freed.
- **Changed (CPU, audit K3):** near its limit the audio now backs off in steps before it cuts sound. After two audio blocks in a row over 85 % of their time it goes up one step: first a moving morph, an FM6 crossing and the valley and wash update less often; then a new note takes over a fading note (the old tail stops when the new note starts) instead of adding a voice; then LIGHT FX. Only after that do fading tails stop early, and a held note only after a block was really missed. A missed block jumps straight to the tails step. It steps down one step after about 1 s in which no block went over 80 % (about 3 s from LIGHT FX up, longer if LIGHT FX was needed again right after it ended), or after about 3 s at a low average load. Measured on the unit twice (the 16-multi GLIDE sweep): no missed blocks and no voices cut, worst block 91 % (b3: one missed block, 17 voices cut, 101 %). Plain DUELL is unchanged.
- **Changed (CPU audit K5):** when nothing sounds, two stages rest: the delay loop, once its whole line has been silent (about 1.5 s; 0.7 s per part in DUEL), and the master's DC blocker, limiter and knee, once the mix and their own states are at zero. The audio is the same sample for sample; only the CPU load at idle drops (host-measured saving only; not yet measured on the unit). Plain DUELL is unchanged.
- **Changed (screen wording and look, UI audit 2026-11-08):** the part is PART 1 / PART 2 everywhere (CONFIRM dialogs, MIDI LEARN); AUTO TIDE says WANDER / JOURNEY (status WANDERING / JOURNEY) and TARGET says BOTH; the LFOs' WAVE reads SINE, TRIANGLE, SQUARE; MENU says HOME OPENS SETTINGS and MULTI LOAD KEEPS / SETS PLAY MODE. Muted values stay grey on PALE, part 2 on GREY no longer looks disabled, MIDI LEARN's footer line is no longer cut, no divider rules, the empty ARP and MORPH pictures share one line, and the CPU pill no longer looks like part of the capsule.

## 1.0.0-b3

Not flashed yet.

- **Fixed (ARP pages, POLY):** while a POLY stretch shortens an ARP SEQ part's loop, the footer step marker on the ARP pages now follows the step that plays (it counted the full loop and drifted off the sound).
- **Fixed (live recording, audit 2026-11-08):** REC no longer stops the sequences, and no longer rewrites an ARP SEQ pattern from the arp's own notes (a held key climbed a MONO pattern to note 127 in three loops, a firm key accented every step). Arp OFF and classic arps record as before.
- **Changed (owner, 2026-11-08):** with TIDE, HOME has no MODE page: its round is MORPH, TIDE SETTINGS, AUTO TIDE. PLAY MODE (LAYER, STEREO, DUEL; the header says PLAY MODE STEREO), XFADE and SPLIT are now the last three rows of TIDE SETTINGS (REC sits between ARC and PLAY MODE). They work as before (OCT+ / OCT- step XFADE by 10 % and SPLIT by an octave; knobs by the clicks turned) (XFADE and SPLIT only in LAYER; elsewhere their values are dim and knob 1's line says LAYER ONLY) and are kept where they were; they are not part of STYLE. HOME OPENS = MODE opens TIDE SETTINGS on the MODE row. Plain DUELL is unchanged.
- **New (live recording, owner 2026-11-08):** on an ARP SEQ part (most factory multis) the keys now record into the pattern the part plays, while it keeps playing: a key sounds as you play it and goes into the nearest step of the arp, and the pattern plays it back as played. A key held across steps ties them. Tapping REC on such a part says ARP SEQ: KEYS RECORD. A key always sets the step's note there (an ARP SEQ step plays one note), whatever REC says; a key held on an armed ARP SEQ part also goes out to MIDI OUT.
- **New:** a REC row on TIDE SETTINGS, ADD (the default) or REPLACE. ADD is how recording worked: a note played into a step that holds notes is added. REPLACE: the first note you play into a step in a pass clears that step's old notes and takes their place; more notes played into the same step in that pass (a chord) are added; steps you do not play into keep their notes. It is not part of any STYLE, is saved with the other settings (an older record loads as ADD) and works on ARP SEQ parts too.
- **Changed:** OCT- + K3 / K4 on AUTO TIDE now say what they set: TIME 16 / WAIT 2 (WAIT OFF), like K1's BACK TO ORIGINAL; they said nothing.
- **Fixed (TIDE POLY):** after you release all keys and play again during a shortened loop, the loop restarts from its first step (it started part-way in).
- **New:** On TIDE builds, HOME's three pages show the busiest audio block of the last 4 seconds (in % of its time) in a small dim pill beside part 1's capsule; a red badge beside part 2's capsule shows audio blocks missed recently; it goes away after a minute with no new miss. ABOUT's PEAK now shows the same busiest-block figure, and the missed-block count counts every missed audio period. No sound changes.
- **Fixed:** once the selected part is armed, a tap of REC disarms it on every page, HOME included, and no longer flips HOLD (it could not be turned off from HOME); the toast says REC OFF. Unarmed on HOME the tap is still the HOLD switch, and its toast now says how to record: HOLD ON: SEQ+REC=REC (hold SEQ, tap REC).
- **Fixed:** the PRESETS knob loads sounds on STEP and PARAMETER LOCKS too (owner: "in SEQ mode I can't change the preset patch"); steps and parameter locks stay. OCT- held + PRESETS (load undo) works there. PATTERNS, MULTIS and TOOLS still ignore it.
- **New (developer tool):** the build fails when a function that the audio path reaches (per block or per sample) sits in the slow XIP flash instead of RAM, unless `felucca-base/tools/ram_allow.py` names it with a reason (CPU audit K6, `tools/hot_reach.py`). The list starts with the functions in flash today, 333 entries at b3 (the glide and crossing functions of audit finding 7 among them); the check itself changes no image and no function was moved to make the list.
- **Changed:** the PATCHES list (the PRESETS knob, the page, every TYPE) has a fixed order (owner, 2026-11-05 and 2026-11-08: "empty user ones go to the end of the list"): the factory sounds first (#1 to #124, then #125 to #171 and any sound added later), then every sound you saved, in slot order (a sound saved over #13 is there too, not at #13), then the empty USER slots (`USER EMPTY`) last. Only the order of the places moves: the numbers and labels (`#13`, `U1`), the slots, the FAV stars, the saved records, the editor and backups are as they were, and MIDI program change keeps its numbers (bank 0 and 1: program n is slot n + 1; bank 2: program n is #129 + n). About 80 B of flash.
- **Fixed:** the factory sounds past the 124 slots (#125 to #171, the drones too) have their number in the footer (`#164 SAND THROAT`) and as a TIDE destination, as PATCHES shows them; they used to show the name alone (owner: "why are some of the predefined sounds non numbered?"). Plain DUELL is unchanged.
- **Fixed:** the web Backup page restore says how many patches lose their FM6 MORPH TO target on a unit that may be plain DUELL (no INFO TIDE tag: plain DUELL 1.1 and the TIDE builds before the tag, stable-2026-11-03 and beta-2026-11-04, look the same). A unit that says it is TIDE keeps the target. The sound and the device are unchanged.
- **New (developer test):** host group `uartflood` (Felucca #258's `timer5_midi_test`): the real TIMER5 handler and TRS parser under a saturated CC stream with 4.5 ms audio renders; clock timestamps stay within 1 ms, no byte dropped. No firmware change.
- **New (developer test):** host group `tcwrap` (audit A1): the two-core job number is 32 bits and wraps after about 36 days of play; the test walks the real hand-over code across the wrap, with stale words in the mailbox, and checks that no job gets number 0 and every answer is still read right.
- **New (developer test):** UI lint scenes for a DAMAGED multi, the ABOUT id and the PATCHES list past #163 (finding 31); `ui_truth_test` also checks the footer number of a sound past #124.
- **Changed (developer note):** `settings_persist.c` says why object 48 stays (finding 33, owner D48). Comment only.
- **Changed (CI):** the `emu` job (the emulator tests under emsdk) now runs and gates the Pages and release-channel refresh (PR #4).
- **Changed (plain DUELL):** the CPU meter changes shared audio code, so the plain DUELL image (`DUELL_TIDE=0`) is no longer byte for byte the b2 one; `jam/off.sha256` is re-pinned (3f69b964 to 161b55d1). The sound is unchanged (host sound tests bit-exact).
- **New (developer test):** host group `hotreach`: `tools/test_hot_reach.py` checks the hot-code check itself on a made-up disassembly; a stale allow-list entry (the function is in RAM now) now fails the build.

## 1.0.0-b2

Tag `tide-1.0.0-b2` (integ merge commit 0d2fcb9). The owner's unit runs the pre-tag build 36f03cd (flashed 2026-11-06), whose ABOUT reads `1.0.0-b2+`. The tagged build differs from it only by 069c65e (the every-other-block blend only while a GLIDE runs; a fader jump over 12 steps is not a move) plus tests and docs; it is not flashed yet, and its ABOUT will read `1.0.0-b2`.

- Eight drones, #164 to #171, two per theme. Desert: SAND THROAT, HORN SWELL. Neon city: NEON BRASS, RAIN STRINGS. Space: VOID SHIMMER, SOLAR WIND. Cyberpunk: GRID HUM, IRON WORKS. Each holds a 3-note chord on the unit with 0 misses (raw peak 77 to 91 %). Sounds added from #164 on are listed in file order after #163, so #125 to #163 and their bank-2 MIDI programs never move.
- **Changed (CPU fix, measured on the unit 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-08):** the real TIDE morph blender now runs from SRAM; the earlier placement list moved only its wrapper. WASH retains its filter coefficients until the note-derived floor changes, and FM6 bridge-only rendering skips unused ARP preparation. The wash arithmetic remains sample-identical to the legacy reference. The unmeasured extra 25% voice-budget margin and the accompanying capacity changes were removed: the calibrated `1.0.0-b1` NOTES limits and release behavior remain the comparison baseline. Host equivalence, target placement and image checks establish the software change; the owner's GLIDE sweep on the unit shows about 3 missed audio blocks per FM6 crossing during a GLIDE, accepted for the betas (KNOWN_LIMITS); the owner heard no clipping.
- **Changed:** with an FM6 part under MORPH the NOTES budget charges the FM6 crossing (the bridge's 76 instead of 7 a voice) while the fader moves or a GLIDE runs, and about a second after; the notes thin during a crossing, then return. At rest the charge is the old 7.
- **Changed:** while a GLIDE runs, the morph is blended every other block (the fader one block, 0.73 ms, behind) and the last blend is written in between; a fader move by hand or MIDI, and a jump over 12 steps (a load), are blended every block as before. The unit's pre-tag build 36f03cd blended a hand move every other block too; the tag blends it every block again, so a hand-moved morph sounds exactly as it did before b2.
- **New (developer tool, test-mode builds only):** the console word `glide 1 [T]` (launch the back-and-forth glide on the selected part's morph target, as knob 1 of the TIDE page does; PLAY starts with it; T is the TIME index 0 to 6) and `glide 0` (stop it), next to `mload N` and `cpu`; `cpu_multis.py --glide [--gtime T]` loads each multi, launches the glide and samples `cpu` once a second.

## 1.0.0-b1

The first versioned build (tag `tide-1.0.0-b1`, 817658d), flashed on the owner's unit 2026-11-05. It holds everything below, from DUELL 1.1 on: the entries of the dated builds listed at the end are not split out.

- **Changed:** MULTIS opens with LOAD picked, as PATTERNS already did: a turn of knob 1 to a multi and one OCT+ loads it, no knob 2 first (OCT- on MULTIS now goes HOME at once, as on PATTERNS). Every entry to a page drops the pick, so a SAVE left picked is LOAD again the next time. TOOLS (CLEAR, INIT, RESTORE) and PARAMETER LOCKS have no LOAD and are unchanged (owner request, wave 14).
- **Changed:** MENU > ABOUT shows TIDE's version (`1.0.0-b1`, from the file `TIDE_VERSION`) beside the date instead of the short commit. A trailing `+` (`1.0.0-b1+`) marks a build that is not exactly the tagged release: the tree has changes, HEAD is not the commit of the tag `tide-<version>`, or there is no git. `DUELL_TIDE_ID` still sets another id. A `DUELL_TIDE=0` build is unchanged (the id is TIDE-only). The installer page and `web/releases.py` read the packages `duell-tide-stable-X.Y.Z.fwsc` and `duell-tide-beta-X.Y.Z-bN.fwsc` (the dated names still count as older).
- **Changed:** the NOTES budget keeps more room for the audio's peaks (CPU overruns, the crackle): its limit is 72 % of a half's time instead of 84 %, because the unit's own per-second peaks run about 30 + 0.74 x the model's figure. On the factory multis a part has one or two notes fewer than before: DUB CHORD 3 FM6 and 4 ANALOG notes (was 4 and 5), BASIC CHORD 5 and 5 (7 and 6), CELLAR TAPE 2 and 7 (3 and 8), NIGHT ORGAN 3 and 4 (4 and 5), ORGAN SKANK 5 and 7 (6 and 8), WARM ECHO and DEEP 332 5 and 5 (7 and 7), MINIMAL 12 5 and 6 (7 and 7), TINE DUB 4 and 4 (5 and 5), PAD SPLIT, WHISPER TIDE and EERIE SIGNAL 3 and 3 (4 and 4), TINE STRINGS 4 and 5 (6 and 6), COLD CHAPEL 4 and 2 (6 and 2), HAZY TENSOR 3 and 4 (5 and 5), FINAL REEL 4 and 4 (5 and 5); a chord held on a pad loses its top note sooner and a release tail is stolen earlier, in exchange for halves that no longer run out of time. The audio interrupt's own shedding (voices faded at 85 % / 100 %) should seldom fire now.
- **Changed:** a released FM6 note ends once its tail is inaudible (its carriers together under -66 dB of a full carrier) and fades out over the last 0.7 ms, instead of running its six operators down to -89 dB and stopping with a step: FM6 tails (BIG STAB, TINE ROOM, the bells) end a little earlier, the voice count between chord hits is lower (DUB CHORD keeps 3.3 of 4 FM6 voices alive on average, not 4) and the small click at the end of a quick release (DUB DRAWBAR) is gone. Part of the CPU-overrun fix.
- **Changed (developer tool):** the factory-multis record tool (`tools/factory_multis.py`, `tests/gen_factory_multis.c`) reads a velocity after a step's note (`0v90A`, 1 to 127, default 100) and refuses a tie after a rest or at step 0, a velocity out of range, and a pattern length other than 8 or 16 (12 needs `poly=True` on the part, 32 needs `bars=2`); `selftest` covers each rule. The 16 records are byte for byte as before. Tools only, no flash change (T1 of the roles and multis spec).
- **New (developer tool):** the host group `tideroles` (`sh host_tests.sh tideroles`, roles spec task T2) runs the 16 factory multis 64 bars each under their own STYLE and the neighbour's, PITCH OFF and ON, each from the boot state, and checks the register lane (no note outside the Original's lane or under C2), onsets a bar per role, that a sustained part gains no onset, motif retention, the 30 to 120 Hz band (30 to 60 Hz for a bass multi) under the full band on PLAY and after JRNY, and loudness within 3 dB of the median; `TIDEROLES_V=1` lists every violation by slot, part, bar, step and note. It fails on today's multis by design (the numbers are the baseline of the roles spec's content tasks) and stays out of the default list until they pass it. Tests only: the image is unchanged.
- **Changed:** JRNY's texture family is now a hidden flag (roles-and-multis spec C3, D39): eight FX sounds (DUST BED, TAPE HISS, MOOR WIND, WHITE OUT, NIGHT DRONE, STORM FRONT, TAPE DRONE, FROZEN AIR) are marked as textures and drones in the factory sound list, and a texture or drone now travels only between those; the other FX (pings, sweeps, bursts) form their own one-shot family. A USER record in the FX category counts as a one-shot. No new PATCHES category and no screen changes; the flag is stored only in TIDE builds, so plain DUELL is byte for byte as before. About 28 B of flash.
- **Changed:** JRNY keeps the starting sound's attack (roles spec C2): a pad (ATK 60 or more) travels to other pads of its role family, a stab (SUS 20 or less) to stabs, keys to keys, so a skank chord never turns into a slow wash nor a pad into a stab; where the family holds no other sound of that attack the part still turns, to any sound of the family. Main-loop code in tide_ui.c only; no screen changes; about 100 B of flash.
- **Changed:** a sustained part holds still in automatic WNDR / JRNY: a pattern with no hit or one hit (a drone, a texture) is never varied by DENS, SYNC, ACC or VOIC, and the phrase edits add no repeat to a part of two hits or fewer, ARC's peak included (roles spec C4; TIDE-only code, the DUELL_TIDE=0 image is unchanged, flash -4 B).

Sounds and patterns are unchanged.

An older settings record loads with the new rows at their defaults.
- **New:** STYLE row at the top of TIDE SETTINGS (DUB, DEEP, MINIMAL, AMBIENT, PEAK, CUSTOM): one move sets the glide MODE, TIME and WAIT and the VARY BARS, TURNS, POLY, SYNC, ARC and WASH rows; a hand edit makes it CUSTOM. A new unit starts on DUB; an older record loads as CUSTOM with its values kept.

A slot you never saved over takes the new pattern at power-on (written once); a slot you saved is never touched.
- **New:** SEQ MORPH (a pattern has an End A and an End B), ARP SEQ on PLAY, KEEP in the SEQ tools; K1 twisted while gliding stops the glide.
- **New:** 32 more factory sounds, #129 to #160, listed after the 128 in PATCHES as read-only places (no slot; SAVE offers USER 1; they can be marked FAV, and bank 2 of MIDI program change reaches them): the 8 voices of the dub techno / house pack (CHORD ECHO, DARK MIN7, ORGAN SKANK, WARM WASH, AIR SHIMMER, SLOW TIDE, SUB PLUCK, DUST BED, each with its End B) and 24 pads, chords, stabs, plucks and textures on ANALOG, WHEEL, PHASE, WAVE and NOISE, so JRNY has more to travel between (engine + category groups of 6 or more sounds: 3 to 6 of 66; of 4 or fewer: 61 to 55). No number of the 128 moves; the 128 are unchanged. About 2.2 KB of flash.

# Changelog of DUELL with TIDE

What changed in the fork (pssdrnk's DUELL-TIDE),. Upstream's own history is in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md); the notes of DUELL 1.1 are in [RELEASE_NOTES.md](RELEASE_NOTES.md).

New lines for the fork go here, not into CHANGELOG.md.

Not an OSCILL/R release: pssdrnk's fork of DUELL 1.1 (<https://github.com/pssdrnk-cmyk/DUELL-TIDE>), adding TIDE, a morph that plays and evolves by itself. Two builds are released and installable from the [installer page](https://pssdrnk-cmyk.github.io/DUELL-TIDE/install/) and the [GitHub releases](https://github.com/pssdrnk-cmyk/DUELL-TIDE/releases): **stable `stable-2026-11-03`** (`187e9d0`) and **beta `beta-2026-11-04`** (`77a6df2`). The entries below cover those builds and the work merged on `jam11` since; that later work is in no build yet (the release notes of each tag say what that build holds). Add a line here for every build flashed to a unit: date, commit, what changed.

- **Changed:** the sound editor says in its footer that it is the stock DUELL 1.1 editor (OSCILL/R) and does not edit the TIDE pages; the landing page's link says the same.
- **Changed:** the first-start guide teaches the basics: its part marker page (2/5) has a SOUNDS AND MORPH note (PRESETS picks a sound, ALGORITHM a part, HOME morphs, hold HOME for the MENU), the PARAMETER LOCK note says arm REC and turn a knob; the part marker notes are shorter. Text only, 8 B smaller.
- **New:** AUTO TIDE in HOME's round, with the modes ONCE, LOOP, DRIFT, WNDR and JRNY (JRNY travels between factory sounds of the part's own engine, FM6 included, and never changes engine); where two unlike sounds cross, a short filter dip.
- **New:** TIDE SETTINGS (first called MORPH SETTINGS) in HOME's round: TARGET (SOUND, SEQ or BOTH), VARY BARS, ROLL (MUSIC, the default, SMART, WILD), ARP, PITCH, WASH LEVEL (OFF by default).
- **New:** TIDE SETTINGS (this page was MORPH SETTINGS) gets TURNS (TAKE TURNS by default: in automatic WNDR / JRNY with both parts, part 2 varies on bars 2 and 6, part 1 on bars 4 and 8) and POLY (OFF / GENTLE / WIDE: one part's loop now and then shorter, back to full at a phrase). An older settings record loads with the new rows at their defaults.
- **New:** STYLE row at the top of TIDE SETTINGS (DUB, DEEP, MINIMAL, AMBIENT, PEAK, CUSTOM): one move sets the glide MODE, TIME and WAIT and the VARY BARS, TURNS, POLY, SYNC, ARC and WASH rows; a hand edit makes it CUSTOM. A new unit starts on DUB; an older record loads as CUSTOM with its values kept.
- **New:** ARC row (OFF / RISE / DROP, OFF by default): a 32 to 64 bar shape in automatic WNDR / JRNY (rise, peak, optional two-bar drop, thinned breakdown, recovery), with the status line showing the phase, and a hand drop: OCT- held + a turn of K2 on the AUTO TIDE page drops at the next phrase.
- **New:** TURNS gains LEAD (call and response: the two parts lead two bars each of four while the other thins out). ECHO, a fourth value that sounded like LEAD, is folded into it: TURNS has three values, PEAK uses LEAD, and a stored ECHO reads as LEAD. The setting byte is the same.
- **New:** SYNC row in TIDE SETTINGS (OFF / SOFT / PUSH, SOFT by default): the automatic pattern edits lean towards the off-beat "and" and a limited push, never onto a beat. The settings record gains a byte; older records load with SYNC SOFT.
- **New:** ten of the 32 factory patterns are now for chords and pads (dub techno, deep house): P01 DUB CHORD, P02 DEEP SHUFFLE, P03 DUB OFFBEAT, P04 HOUSE STAB, P05 DOT DELAY, P10 MINIMAL 12 (12 steps, for POLY), P13 DEEP 332, P15 PAD BREATH, P17 HALF PAD and P24 DUB PROG (they replace MORODER OCT, ACID LINE, OFFBEAT, TECHNO RUMBL, TRANCE ROLL, FUNK 16TH, GALLOP, BOOGIE BASS, SUB DROP and TRANCE GATE; the old P29 PAD BREATH is TIED ROOTS). Offsets from C4 as before, so a CHORD TYPE key and ARP SEQ move the whole chord; room left for TIDE. A pattern can carry its own SWING and GATE. A slot you never saved over takes the new pattern at power-on (written once); a slot you saved is never touched.
- **New:** SEQ MORPH (a pattern has an End A and an End B), ARP SEQ on PLAY, KEEP in the SEQ tools; K1 twisted while gliding stops the glide.
- **New:** 32 more factory sounds, #129 to #160, listed after the 128 in PATCHES as read-only places (no slot, no FAV; SAVE offers USER 1): the 8 voices of the dub techno / house pack (CHORD ECHO, DARK MIN7, ORGAN SKANK, WARM WASH, AIR SHIMMER, SLOW TIDE, SUB PLUCK, DUST BED, each with its End B) and 24 pads, chords, stabs, plucks and textures on ANALOG, WHEEL, PHASE, WAVE and NOISE, so JRNY has more to travel between (engine + category groups of 6 or more sounds: 3 to 6 of 66; of 4 or fewer: 61 to 55). No number of the 128 moves; the 128 are unchanged. About 2.2 KB of flash.
- **New:** JRNY stays on each part's own engine (FM6 included) and picks within the starting sound's role family: pads and keys; bass; lead and pluck; hits; FX and textures (a sound alone in its family rolls a new End B of itself). It remembers each part's last four destinations and skips them where the choice allows.
- **New:** USER slots: voice slots 125 to 128 (U1 to U4) and pattern slots P29 to P32 are empty places for new work (`USER EMPTY` until saved); the factory sounds and patterns they stand in for stay listed after them as read-only places. No record, address or capacity changes; a part pointed at an empty USER slot still counts as a factory sound for JRNY, WNDR and the End B roll.
- **New:** MULTIS and PATTERNS are plain lists with the picked entry's preview in a panel at the bottom: both parts' sound names, ARP / SEQ / OFF and the first 16 steps (MULTIS), the pattern's steps (PATTERNS), or NO SEQUENCE; the loaded entry is starred, and only the card OCT+ acts on shows the key. A multi record that does not decode reads DAMAGED.
- **New:** a polish pass on the TIDE screens: AUTO TIDE with both parts shows the faders in two columns (SOUND, SEQ; a row per part), nothing touches the step bars or the margins, MONO draws the glide's path; the MULTIS / PATTERNS badges are keycap pills; the splash and ABOUT text reads clearly over the wave lines.
- **Fixed (TIDE):** JRNY keeps pads and keys in their role: the 11 sounds filed under OTHER are re-filed (TEMPLE BLOCK, PIXEL KICK, GRAVEL SNARE, VOCAL BEATS, TABLA TALK as PERC; GLASS SHARDS, DEAD BATTERY, DIAL TONE, GEIGER, MODEM as FX; DUSTY DRONE as PAD; sounds and list places unchanged), FX is its own JRNY family apart from the hits, a sound alone in its family no longer jumps to any sound of the engine, and a part playing a USER record walks its own category's family. Levels: GHOST HARP, FLOOR THUD, WOW FLUTTER and TABLA TALK re-trimmed; M16 CELLAR TAPE's chord stabs are 16 steps like its bass (test groups `tidejrnyfamily`, `sounds`).
- **Fixed (sounds):** #65 ARP PLUCK and #56 SINGING BOWL were too quiet (5 and 2.5 dB under their category) and could not be trimmed up. ARP PLUCK's ladder filter rings less and its 7 kHz chirp is gone (now a warm pluck around 700 Hz, 4 dB louder); SINGING BOWL bows longer and rings with less damping (3 dB louder). Both pass the sound audit without an exemption (test group `sounds`).
- **Changed (sounds):** aftertouch means one thing per kind of sound in the 128: on a bass it adds drive (SATURATION), on leads, pads, keys and plucks it opens the filter (CUT, or TWIN CUTOFF / SHAPE where the multimode filter is open), at the same depth everywhere. It used to reach 13 different targets among the basses alone. FX and drum sounds keep theirs (test group `sounds` checks the convention).
- **Changed (sounds):** the slow movement of the pads, keys and FX in the 128 follows the tempo: a CYCLE slower than a second and every LFO at 1 Hz or slower now run in 1, 2 or 4 bars (the nearest at 120 BPM to the free time they had), so two parts and the drum machine no longer drift against each other. 52 sounds; vibrato and faster motion stay free (test group `sounds` checks it).
- **Changed (TIDE, sounds):** MORPH towards a factory sound's End B (with TIDE always the MUSICAL roll of the sound's seed) now moves with the kind of sound: 84 of the 160 sounds have a new seed, chosen so that basses, leads, pads, plucks and keys open up towards B (bass 14 of 24 brighter by 10 % or more, was 4; pads 15 of 22, was 7; leads 12 of 19, was 7) and no B jumps in level any more (BIG STAB -7.5 dB, HAN SOLO -7.0, ALIEN CHIMES -8.3, NYLON GUT +6.3 and 11 more are inside +-2.5 dB now). FRETLESS, ISLAND STEEL and MALLET WOOD still change level towards B (no seed keeps them steady). Plain DUELL plays the stored End B values and is unchanged (test group `sounds` now judges every End B the fork plays).
- **New (sounds):** three FM6 dub voices after the 160, read-only places like them: FM HAZE (a soft FM pad, its low-pass breathing over four bars, wide chorus, long room), DUB DRAWBAR (an FM drawbar organ, top cut, slow chorus, dub delay) and BELL STAB (a dark FM bell, low-passed, into a dotted delay and a plate). All three cut their lows for the bass gear. The places after #128 renumber by category (#129 to #163; docs/PATCH_NUMBERS.md); no number of the 128 moves. About 220 B of flash.
- **Fixed (TIDE):** the AUTO TIDE step bars show what plays: an ARP SEQ part's playhead and its silent steps, ties after a note that did not sound, POLY's shorter loop, patterns that are not a whole number of bars, and chance steps (striped until they have rolled, then solid or a dim outline). The page and the audio use one step function (test group `tidesteps`).
- **Fixed (TIDE):** the pages tell the truth: STEP's playhead follows an ARP SEQ part's own step and its steps past a POLY-shortened loop are dim; STYLE back onto CUSTOM brings your last nine values back (one OCT press no longer loses them); on TIDE SETTINGS the TURNS, POLY, SYNC and ARC values are dim while MODE, ROLL or TARGET make them do nothing, and knob 1's line says what is missing; AUTO TIDE's ARC line keeps the trip ("RISE 3/6  B 5/16"); TURNS LEAD's message fits the header. The layout lint now sweeps AUTO TIDE and TIDE SETTINGS in their busy states.
- **Fixed (TIDE):** after STOP a SEQ arp on a latched chord (HOLD ARP, as in M02 and M04) kept playing. It now waits for PLAY and starts again from the top; STOP ALL still ends the latch, and keys you hold still play (test group `stophang`).
- **Fixed (TIDE):** ARC resumes where it was after STOP and PLAY (a load or a K1 stop starts it again at RISE); a multi or pattern loaded mid-session starts as written (no generation or pitch edit carries over) and a pattern edit drops the pitch offsets of the old steps; HOME also clears the pitch edits of End B; the chord voicing of VOIC runs with PITCH OFF too (the bass of a chord stays); PITCH never walks a bass note under C3 or a BASS-category sound and does nothing on SCALE CHR; a load in mid-trip puts the loaded parts back at A and the running tide starts its trip again from there.
- **Fixed (TIDE):** the automatic pattern edits keep the motif: an offbeat skank's "and"s (a pattern's own strongest place in the beat) are anchors like the beats, every bar keeps at least 3/5 of its stored hits wherever DENS, the phrase gaps, LEAD or the ARC breakdown thin it, and a hit never drifts more than two 16ths; LEAD's call and response is for lead and pluck lines only (pads, basses and chord skanks play whole), and the tide gets dynamics without a setting: a little louder in ARC's late rise and peak, softer in the breakdown and on a LEAD answer's bars (test group `tidemotif`).
- **Fixed (TIDE):** a hard key (velocity above 110) opens the filter in UNISON too: the accent read the voice's velocity, which UNISON lowers to keep the level, so it never fired there (audit P13).
- **Fixed (TIDE):** ANALOG's DRV fades the saturation in over its first eight notches: DRV 0 to 1 used to switch the full tanh on and drop the level by 0.7 dB at once (audit P14).
- **Fixed (TIDE):** a sequencer lock or automation on PATCH TRIM, the mixer level or a send no longer clicks: the plain mix ramps the gain across the block like it does for a matrix route (a lock doubling TRIM on a held sine stepped 76 to 124 times the steady slope at the block seam, now 2 to 5) (audit S10).
- **Fixed (TIDE):** the matrix offers the LFO levels again, as LFO 1 LEVEL and LFO 2 LEVEL (destinations DEP1 / DEP2, the same as the LEVEL knobs of the LFO pages): they were hidden in 1.0 although they always ran and two factory patches route aftertouch to LFO 2's. Stored routes sound as before (audit S15, decision D20).
- **Fixed (TIDE):** an ARP SEQ part started with PLAY and no key held plays the part's chord from C4 (CHRD MIN7: C Eb G Bb), not a single C4; with CHRD OFF it is still the one note. The first start (M04 DUB CHORD) now stabs a chord before any key is pressed.
- **Fixed (TIDE):** a triplet step or arp RATE (8T, 16T, 4T, 2T, 32T) is no longer swung: SWG moved the offbeat of a triplet pair to 70 % and more; triplets now stay straight, 1/8 and 1/16 keep their swing (audit S14).
- **Fixed (TIDE):** the ARP pages' picture draws the arp's own notes: it started at the second step, so its first column showed the second note; the picture and the arp now share one note-order function (audit U06).
- **Fixed (TIDE):** ARP TARGET with ARP SEQ honours a TIE like the note arp does: the units' gate stays open through the TIE steps (they closed it, as a rest) (audit G07).
- **Fixed (TIDE):** a LINKed filter 2 (and NOISE's loudness make-up) now closes with filter 1 during a crossing or a GLIDE's breath, so a PARALLEL twin-filter sound no longer keeps its filter-2 colour through the dip; the effective cutoff is made in one helper (uc_eff, test group tidecrossings).
- **Fixed (TIDE):** a TIDE crossing's WASH no longer fades out where it is most wanted: with the part's level 24 dB or more over the noise the gain wrapped to zero instead of stopping at x 16 (test group `audiopolish`).
- **Fixed (TIDE):** with WASH OFF (the default) a sounding part no longer measures its power or generates the wash noise on every block, in a transition or out of it (test group `audiopolish`).
- **Fixed (TIDE):** the defined-arithmetic pass is finished in the effects, the LOFI / TRIO / FORMANT engines, the click and the voice filter weights (left shifts of negative values written as multiplications: the same code is generated), the host rig's NPART=4 no longer indexes past the two shown parts, and the new test group `ubsanrender` renders the 242 scenes, the multis and the 160 factory sounds under UBSan.
- **Fixed (TIDE):** FM6 UNISON: when a voice ends or one is refused the others no longer step by about 1 dB at the block line; the block ramps from the gain of the block before to the new one (test group `audiopolish`).
- **Fixed (TIDE):** a new Original no longer drains part 2's FM6 crossing stand-ins from the main loop while core 1 still holds that part's job after an overrun; the render side does it on its next block (test group `twocore`).
- **New:** the seven factory multis that had no sequence (M03, M05, M06, M07, M08, M13, M16) have one, and M10 to M12 their own lines; M16 is CELLAR TAPE (dub techno), the STRESS TEST is now only a test scene.
- **New:** live graphs (`ui_live.c`): AMP ENV, ENV 2, FM6 OP ENV, the DEST pages, LFO 1 / 2, CYCLE ENV, the MULTI and TWIN filters, INSERT sweeps, the MATRIX and CHORD pages move a marker on the drawn line, about 15 a second, with no look taken above 80 % CPU; MENU > ANIMATION OFF keeps them still. An LFO 2 or CYCLE that nothing reads runs a silent preview, captioned PREVIEW.
- **Fixed (also with `DUELL_TIDE=0`):** MODE / SPLIT stays OFF when turned left at OFF; the external MIDI clock keeps its phase across tempo changes; FM6 live operator level; UNISON spread; PHYS string tuning; an FM6 voice under MORPH ends after its release; undefined arithmetic in MORPH, FM6, PHYS and WAVE (UBSan clean); End B kept by rename and FM6 PUT; the CHORD keys' TYPE / VOICING no longer reset to OFF by loading a sound (the CHORD page says NEEDS POLY or SET A SCALE when the keys cannot play the chord, K4 is the part's VOICE); GLO pages: ALGORITHM picks the part (MIXER included). Each fix has a host test.
- **Fixed (also with `DUELL_TIDE=0`):** the arp on the internal clock keeps its tempo (each step used to be rounded up to a whole audio block: 8 steps took 8.04 sixteenths and the arp fell a 1/16 behind the sequencer after about 14 bars at 120 BPM); ARP SWING puts the offbeat late (it landed early: SWG 75 at 30 % of the pair, now 70 %); ARP PROB 0 never plays (it played 1 step in 128). Test group `arptiming`.
- **Fixed (also with `DUELL_TIDE=0`):** an FM6 note struck again while it still sounds (the same key twice, a steal, an arp or TIDE step on a held chord) goes on from the level it has instead of falling to silence and attacking from 0; pads and brass with a slow attack no longer click or dip (test group `fm6`).
- **Fixed (also with `DUELL_TIDE=0`):** the first-start guide teaches what the unit does: QUICK EDIT no longer lists SEL, and PARAMETER LOCK says to arm REC on STEP and turn a knob (the OCT+ gesture went in 1.0). QUICKSTART, VOICE_CONTROLS and SOUND_RECIPES are corrected against the firmware (page and knob names, the menu list, the unison width of about 23.5 cents).
- **Fixed:** TIDE's settings are in every backup: the 12 fields moved from patch chunk 21 into the settings record's reserved tail (an earlier build's record is read once; an older backup leaves them as they are; a newer build's extra fields are kept). The editor says STORE FULL (rc 6, INFO 58 01 = the 126 records TIDE's store takes) instead of 'flash write failed', and a restore writes the removals first, reports every failed object and still restores the other groups.
- **Under the hood:** Huffman-coded fonts, unreached UI render paths removed, the RAM load images packed as one LZMA stream at boot (about 24.9 KB of flash free in the stable build; about 4.1 KB with everything up to the next beta). `DUELL_TIDE=0` builds DUELL 1.1 without any TIDE code; `jam/check_off.sh` checks it. Every build checks each call relocation against its exact reach (instead of one bound for hot code); the host tests run on Windows (a real `fork()` in `jam/wincompat`), and `jam/check.sh` counts only a nonzero FAIL line as a failure.
- **New:** MENU > ABOUT shows the short commit of a TIDE build beside the version (`DUELL_TIDE_ID` sets another id), so a stable and a beta unit can be told apart (48 B of flash; audit F-22).
- **Fixed (also with `DUELL_TIDE=0`):** the DIGITAL and PING-PONG delay no longer click when its time changes (TIME, TEMPO, a BPM step, an external clock): the read point slides, a tape's bend, instead of jumping (the HF burst of a TIME change fell from -7 to -40 dB against the tone); and an external MIDI clock's tempo is measured once a beat over a rolling four-beat window, so a steady clock no longer moves the delay time or the TEMPO display at every 16th (test groups `dlyglide`, `clockdrift`). RANDOM and OCT UP are unchanged.
- **Fixed (also with `DUELL_TIDE=0`):** with ENV SYNC on, a long release (4 BARS at 69 BPM or slower, 3 BARS at 52 BPM or slower) never ended: the voice stayed active at about -71 dB, rendering and holding a pool slot. The release now ends when its step rounds to zero, for ENV 1 and ENV 2 (test group `step3`).
- **Fixed (also with `DUELL_TIDE=0`):** a sound load can be undone again: OCT- held and a turn of PRESETS puts back the last load (a sound, a pattern, a SEQ tool or a lock edit); the same again redoes it. It works wherever PRESETS loads and is not on the TIDE pages, where OCT- + a knob is the STYLE reset. PRESETS now steps like PATCHES' K1 on every page (a fast turn with KNOB ACCEL ON loads only where it settles), and a list of more than 24 entries (the matrix DST) accelerates. The 5 KB undo copy had no gesture since SAVE held became SAVE OVER (test group `uitest`).
- **Fixed (also with `DUELL_TIDE=0`):** a MIDI Start now arms the sequencer and step 0 (and the metronome's first click) sounds on the first clock after it, the MIDI downbeat, instead of on the Start byte, up to a master's Start-to-clock gap early; and the arp plays the velocity of the key that started it (the newest key's for several keys and CHORD; an ACCENT step still 127) instead of a fixed 100, on the voice and on MIDI out (test groups `clockdrift`, `midictl`, `arptiming`).
- **Fixed (also with `DUELL_TIDE=0`):** a delay time too long for the line (1/2 at 77 BPM, 2D at 71 BPM) halves to fit in every mode, as PING-PONG and SPLIT did, instead of being cut to 65,535 samples off the beat in DIGITAL, RANDOM and OCT UP (M05 WHISPER TIDE was 4.6 % late, M13 COLD CHAPEL 41 % short), and the DELAY card names the time it plays; RESONATOR's combs are tuned for the damping low-pass's delay, so a dark RESO no longer rings up to 26 cents flat (test groups `dlyglide`, `resotune`).
- **Fixed (also with `DUELL_TIDE=0`):** GLIDE does what GLD shows, up to 10 s: TIME takes that time for any interval, RATE moves 64 semitones in it (an octave in 12/64), so the top of the knob is no longer all alike; a glide used to move at least 1/16 semitone a block (a semitone in at most about 12 ms) and TIME rounded its steps. ARP TARGET's moves follow GLD's RATE the same way. The 31 factory sounds with GLIDE and the gliding parts of the factory multis M01, M08 and M14 keep the glide they had: their GLD moves to the value that shows their old time (an old 7-semitone glide of 81 / 41 / 28 / 20 ms). User patches saved with GLIDE now glide for the displayed time (no migration of user records).
- **Fixed (also with `DUELL_TIDE=0`):** the filters are exact below 100 Hz: the multimode filter and the other trapezoidal SVFs (TWIN LP / BP / HP / NOTCH) lost the low-pass term and most of the resonance at low cutoffs (RES 127 peaked +10 dB at 41 Hz instead of +16.8, and a steady tone left a DC offset of about -30 dBFS). Their coefficients now keep up to 7 more bits where the cutoff is low (160 B of flash); every filtered factory sound changes a little at its low end.
- **Fixed (also with `DUELL_TIDE=0`):** the CUT dial no longer zippers: turning CUT or sending CC74 moved the filter 1.32 semitones at a block edge; the cutoff now follows the dial in about 3 ms, landing exactly (a jump of 8 steps or more, as a sound load, is not slewed; 28 B of flash).
- **Fixed (also with `DUELL_TIDE=0`):** RES on the multimode filter is resonance over the whole knob: its damping now falls by the same ratio at every step (the peak over the passband at RES 32 / 64 / 96 / 127: 1 / 6 / 11 / 17 dB, was 0 / 1 / 5 / 17; a peak from RES 17 on, where nothing below 48 had one), the ends as before (Q 0.5 to 6.9). The factory sounds and multis keep their resonance: every RES (A and End B), FILTER ENV to RES and matrix FRES depth moves to the step of the same damping (within 1.4 %; a depth's end within 2.1 %). MORPH's and TIDE's RES ceilings and bands move the same way (a roll stays under the resonance it allowed before), and tools/convert_twin.py writes the new steps. User patches are not migrated: RES now does what its place on the knob says.
- **Fixed (also with `DUELL_TIDE=0`):** the CHORUS and the delay MODULATION stay in chorus territory: above the chorus's default rate (CRT 0.41 Hz) its sweep shrinks as the rate rises, so DEPTH sets a pitch swing that holds at any rate (CDP 60 +-18 cents, 127 +-38; CDP 60 was +-64 cents at 1.4 Hz and +-2.5 semitones at 5.4 Hz); at and below 0.41 Hz the chorus is as before. MDEPTH 100 swings the delay +-25 cents and holds that above 0.51 Hz (was +-10 ms: +-55 cents at 0.51 Hz, +-3.7 semitones at 3.4 Hz). The factory multis' MDEPTH moves to the value of the same swing (M02 25 -> 54, M04 17 -> 37, M06 23 -> 50, M07 28 -> 61; a CHANCE in RANDOM / OCT UP is kept); M08 HAZY TENSOR's 18 Hz chorus (+-6.6 semitones) takes CDP 127, the nearest the new law has (+-38 cents).
- **Fixed (also with `DUELL_TIDE=0`):** the master no longer bends a clean mix: a soft knee replaces the always-on tanh (linear up to the limiter's threshold, 1 to 3 % distortion at -8 dBFS gone, about 0.8 dB louder at the limiter), and SPACE +100 pushes its sends 3 dB less (x 1.13 instead of x 1.6, send floors 3 dB lower), so the limiter works less. This re-makes every golden render (audit R05, R06).
- **Fixed (also with `DUELL_TIDE=0`):** COMP stops lifting what is under the threshold: its automatic makeup is half what it was (a quiet tail or noise floor came up by up to +12 dB, now at most +6 dB), and the attack and release now follow the printed times in dB (RELEASE 200 ms took 370 ms to cover 63 % of a 20 dB recovery, now 200 ms). Plain COMP at 1:1 is unchanged (audit R12).
- **Fixed (also with `DUELL_TIDE=0`):** reverb and delay tails end in exact silence: ROOM at DAMP 124 and up, RESO at DAMP 127 and the delay loop (every mode) held a DC of 2 to 64 LSB for ever (a rounding dead band in their one-pole and loop gain). Inaudible, but it rules out skipping a silent bus (audit R08).
- **Under the hood (also with `DUELL_TIDE=0`):** the effect code shares what it had written twice or three times (the master limiter's follower, the reverb tail after each delay path, the reverb's fade-out, SHIM's two 1-2-1 low-passes): the sound is bit for bit the same, the image 648 B smaller (audit R13).
- **Fixed (also with `DUELL_TIDE=0`):** the part EQ no longer clicks when a band's gain moves a step (MORPH and TIDE move it in whole dB): the coefficients glide from the old to the new over the block instead of replacing each other at once. A 1 dB step under a loud 60 Hz tone left a third-difference spike of 28 / 197 / 8970 LSB in LOW / MID / HIGH; now 6 / 8 / 197 (test group `eqglide`, audit R15).
- **Changed (also with `DUELL_TIDE=0`):** the delay's repeats lose their bass: a low cut of about 165 Hz in the feedback loop (a 60 Hz note loses 25 dB over two repeats where a 1 kHz tone loses 6.6), so a dub delay no longer piles up low end under the bass line. FDBK 101 to 120 climbs faster to a loop gain of 1.05 (FDBK 118 and up pass unity), a controlled dub runaway: a repeat that keeps growing until a soft knee in the loop holds it near -3 dBFS, and FDBK 100 and below are as before apart from the low cut. Every sound with a delay changes (test group `dlyfb`, audit E2, owner D21).
- **Under the hood (also with `DUELL_TIDE=0`):** the per-part arrays `byp_m` and `mxs` are sized by the number of parts instead of 2, so the four-part host rig no longer indexes past them; a new test group `bounds` runs the golden renders with array-bounds checking (audit U09). The unit's image is unchanged.
- **Changed (also with `DUELL_TIDE=0`):** ROOM is now a short, diffuse, stereo room: it runs the HALL's code with a short decay (RT60 about 0.25 s at SIZE 0, 0.6 s at 64, 1.3 s at 127, where the old ROOM gave 1.4 s at SIZE 90) instead of the four-comb mono reverb, which rang at a pitch (ringing 7.2 dB RMS against 1.2 dB, left and right correlation 1.00 against -0.10). SHIM keeps the old four-comb room as its base, its PITCH DOWN and MOD are untouched. Sounds that use ROOM change (audit R09 and R10, owner D24; test group `reverb`).
